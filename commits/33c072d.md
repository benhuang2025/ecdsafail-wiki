## Summary

This commit trades Toffoli for qubits. The peak goes from 1309 to 1285 and
average Toffoli goes up by 27,748, so the score drops by 449,108. Two changes
lower the peak:

1. In the x-tail square, the `·2^22 mod p` step used a shifter that keeps 24
   flag qubits alive (a 22-bit spill, `ovf` and `flag_inv`). It is replaced by
   22 modular doublings and, after the subtract, 22 modular halvings. These
   compute the same value in place with one transient qubit. This costs about
   4,800 Toffoli.
2. In the dialog-GCD walk body (background in [8316db1](8316db1.md)), the top
   23 carries of the controlled subtract/add are now computed in place with
   textbook Cuccaro MAJ/UMA gates, instead of on 23 borrowed carry lanes erased
   by measurement. The body needs 23 fewer scratch lanes. Each in-place bit
   costs one extra Toffoli, about 22,850 Toffoli over the four walks.

The branch-comparator cap goes back from 49 to 50 bits (+108 Toffoli) to make
a clean nonce easier to find, and the tail nonce was re-hunted.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

### 1. Shift-by-22 walk in the square (`ROUND84_SHIFT22_WALK_DOUBLE=1`)

- The x-tail (`round84_emit_fused_square_xtail`) runs
  `squaring_sub_from_acc_schoolbook_lowq_shift22` in `arith/multiply.rs`
  (`ROUND84_XTAIL_SCHOOLBOOK` and `ROUND84_XTAIL_KARATSUBA` are not on). It
  computes `acc −= x² mod p`. It squares `x` into a 512-bit `lo | hi`, then
  folds `hi · 2^256 ≡ hi · (2^32 + 977)` into `acc` as a sequence of
  `mod_sub_qq` / `mod_add_qq` of `hi` at shifts 0, 4, 6, 10 and 32 (see
  [modular-fold](../concepts/modular-fold.md)). The jump from shift 10 to 32 is
  a `·2^22`.
- Old: `mod_shift_left_by_k_lowq(hi, 22)`, then `mod_sub_qq`, then
  `mod_shift_right_by_k_lowq`. The shifter rotates 22 top bits into a fresh
  `spill` register and folds them back with five padded Cuccaro adds and two
  constant adds. It leaves `spill` (22), `ovf` and `flag_inv` alive across the
  subtract, so the inverse can clear them. That is 24 extra live qubits at the
  square's high-water mark.
- New, in both `squaring_sub_from_acc_schoolbook` and
  `squaring_sub_from_acc_schoolbook_lowq_shift22`: if
  `ROUND84_SHIFT22_WALK_DOUBLE=1`, do 22 × `mod_double_inplace_direct_const_fast(hi)`,
  then `mod_sub_qq(acc, hi)`, then 22 × `mod_halve_inplace_direct_const_fast(hi)`.
  Each double rotates `hi` up by one bit into a single transient `ovf`, adds the
  sparse constant `2^256 − p` under `ovf`, and clears `ovf` from the parity.
  Each halve is its exact inverse. Nothing stays alive between them.
- `x · 2^22 mod p` equals 22 doublings mod p, so the value is identical. The
  44 constant adds cost more than the shifter's padded adds. By subtraction
  from the measured total (see below), the walk costs about +4,788 Toffoli.
- A comment already in the base (left over from before the [69bd3d7](69bd3d7.md)
  rewrite) describes the same swap: "the block never parks the 24 persistent
  flags … that pinned the square phase". The 24 matches the measured −24.

### 2. Suffix-hosted carries in the GCD body (`DIALOG_GCD_SELECTED_BODY_GATE_SUFFIX_CARRIES=23`)

- The walk's controlled subtract and add (`dialog_gcd_controlled_sub_selected`
  and `..._add_selected` in `rounds/dialog/mod.rs`) use the no-carry-in body
  `cuccaro_sub_fast_borrowed_carries_no_cin` /
  `cuccaro_add_fast_borrowed_carries_no_cin` in `arith/adder.rs`. Before, it
  needed `n − 1` borrowed clean carry lanes, plus `n` lanes for the gated
  operand: `2·body_len − 1` scratch lanes in all. Each carry costs one CCX on
  the way up and is erased on the way down by `hmr` plus a classically
  controlled `cz_if`, with no Toffoli
  ([measurement-uncompute](../concepts/measurement-uncompute.md)).
- New: `selected_body_gate_suffix_carries(n) = min(23, n − 2)`. The low
  `borrowed = n − 1 − suffix` carries still use borrowed lanes. The top
  `suffix` positions use textbook `maj` / `uma` (or `inv_uma` / `inv_maj` for the
  subtract), which keep the carry in the operand register itself. There is one
  CCX in MAJ and one in UMA, so 2 Toffoli per bit instead of 1.
- The scratch planners ask for fewer lanes:
  `dialog_gcd_selected_body_nocin_carry_need(body_len) = body_len − 1 − suffix`
  and `..._scratch_need = body_len + carry_need`. These are used in both selected
  bodies and in `dialog_gcd_build_composite_scratch` (`rounds/dialog/compressed.rs`).
  Where the borrowed pool ran short and the planner allocated fresh qubits,
  23 fewer are allocated at the widest steps.
- Cost: summing `min(23, body_len − 2)` over the 258 steps, with the
  `active_width` schedule and the second-half trim from [004ffc0](004ffc0.md),
  gives 5,713 extra Toffoli per walk, 22,852 over the four walks.

### 3. Other knobs in `configure_ecdsafail_submission_route()`

- `DIALOG_GCD_COMPARE_BITS`: 49 → 50. This undoes [8e13587](8e13587.md): 27 steps
  get their 50th comparator bit back, +108 Toffoli (27 × 4 walks). The comment
  says the new stream needed it for a clean island.
- `DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE`: 251235 → 73612.
- Removes one blank line in `mod.rs`.

### Totals

+22,852 (suffix carries) + 108 (comparator) + 4,788 (shift walk, by
subtraction) = +27,748, the measured change. The peak falls by 24, which
matches the 24 flags removed from the square. The GCD-walk body also had to
come down, because the base comments put that phase at 1309 too. The 23 lanes
that suffix hosting saves are what lets it fall with the square. This note
does not identify the phase that binds at 1285.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

Both qubit cuts are exact:

- The doubling walk computes `hi · 2^22 mod p` exactly, and the halvings undo
  it exactly. `hi` is restored before the closing halvings and the inverse
  square.
- MAJ/UMA is the standard reversible Cuccaro carry. It gives the same sum as
  the borrowed-lane form and leaves no garbage.

The only probabilistic change is the comparator cap, and it is *looser* than
the base (50 against 49). The walk-width envelope, the body trim and the
22-bit apply-clean comparator are unchanged, so the hazard classes are the
same as in [88079e6](88079e6.md). The new op stream needed a re-hunted nonce:
`73612` draws a test set with 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures over
all 9,024 shots. See [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

Both changes remove live ancilla at the peak (the shifter's persistent flags,
and borrowed carry lanes) and pay for it in Toffoli. That is register and scratch
allocation, ancilla-lifetime. The runner-up is a primitive swap in the adders
(Arithmetic Primitive Substitution), because the mechanism is choosing a
different adder and shifter implementation. The goal and effect are the qubit
peak, though.

## Reproduce

1. **Start from the base.** Clone https://github.com/ecdsafail/ecdsafail-challenge
   and run `git checkout 88079e6 -- src/point_add`. The base is the git
   parent. `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol 88079e6 33c072d -- src/point_add` is
   111 lines in 5 files.
2. **Apply the patch below** from the repo root with `git apply --ignore-whitespace`.
   The flag lets the patch apply whether the files use LF or CRLF line
   endings. It is the complete diff against the base.
3. **Check the tree.** `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol 33c072d -- src/point_add`
   should print nothing.
4. **Run the benchmark.** Run `./setup.sh && ./benchmark.sh` with no
   `DIALOG_*` or `ROUND84_*` environment variables set, because
   `set_default_env` only fills in unset variables. Expect:
   - 1285 qubits
   - 1,532,135 average Toffoli (rounded)
   - score 1,968,793,475
   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures

The score only reproduces with `DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE=73612` and an identical op
stream. Any other change needs a new nonce hunt.
