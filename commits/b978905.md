## Summary

Earlier commits narrowed the GCD register widths `SCHED_J2` and compare windows
`GAP_J2` by editing the baked tables: 1 bit over divsteps 101–260
([`2aedb45`](2aedb45.md)), 2 bits over 133–260 ([`3222da6`](3222da6.md)), and 2
bits over 101–260 ([`60d6185`](60d6185.md)). This commit restores the original
`a254a8a` tables. Instead it subtracts a runtime per-divstep depth from both
tables: 1 bit on divsteps 85–156, 2 bits on 157–204 and 3 bits on 205–260.

The author measured that 1-bit cuts spread over many steps buy more Toffoli per
unit of λ (the expected number of failing shots per draw) than deep cuts, and
that 2-bit cuts pay best only around divsteps 181–197. A staircase that starts
early and deepens toward the end is the best placement. The note says it gives
lower Toffoli and lower λ than 3222da6's flat cut. The narrowed width must
never increase from one step to the next, so suffix staircases are the only
legal shape.

With the caps at 1152 the peak is 1152. Against `2aedb45` (1152 qubits) this
saves 8,626 Toffoli. Against the frontier `7936360` (1154 × 1,294,261) it gives
up 1,746 Toffoli but saves 2 qubits, and the product is 577k lower.

## What changed and why it saves Toffoli

1. **Runtime narrowing profile, `src/point_add/trailmix_ludicrous/gcd.rs`.**
   - New `tail_narrow_at(i)` returns how many bits to remove at divstep `i`.
   - With `TLM_TN_SPEC` set, the spec is a list of `lo-hi:depth` segments over
     half-open divstep ranges, and later segments win. Otherwise `TLM_TN_D`,
     `TLM_TN_E` and `TLM_TN_MODE` (`flat`, `up`, `down`) give a depth `d` over the
     last `e` steps. Those are unset here.
   - The depth is clamped so no register goes below 4 bits. The clamp does not
     bind for the shipped spec.
   - New `sched_j2_at(i) = max(1, SCHED_J2[i] − tail_narrow_at(i))` is now
     `current_n` in both `forward_gcd_jump` and `reverse_gcd_jump`.
   - `cmp_window()` starts from `GAP_J2[i] − tail_narrow_at(i)`. Width and window
     move together, so the lowest compared bit stays where it was (see
     [`2aedb45`](2aedb45.md)).
2. **Tables back to `a254a8a`, `trailmix_ludicrous/schedule.rs`.** `SCHED_J2` and
   `GAP_J2` go up by 1 at indices 101–260 against `2aedb45`. That undoes its
   narrowing, so all narrowing now comes from the spec.
3. **Spec and caps, `src/point_add/mod.rs`, `configure_q1153_second512_submission_defaults()`.**
   - `TLM_TN_SPEC = "85-157:1,157-205:2,205-261:3"`.
   - `TLM_TARGET_Q` 1151 → 1152 and `TLM_SQUARE_PEAK_CAP` 1151 → 1152.
   - `trailmix_ludicrous/mod.rs::install_q1153_submission_defaults` changes its
     own `TLM_TARGET_Q` 1151 → 1150. It skips names that are already set, and
     the `mod.rs` default runs first, so the effective cap is 1152.
4. **What the profile removes.** Against the `a254a8a` tables it removes 336
   bit-steps per walk: 72 × 1 + 48 × 2 + 56 × 3. The flat cut of 3222da6 removed
   128 × 2 = 256, and 60d6185's 160 × 2 = 320. The effective width stays
   monotone, for example …, 182, 180, … at step 85 and 11, 9, 8, 8, 8, 8 at the
   end. Every walk add, compare and cswap at the narrowed steps gets cheaper,
   in all four walks. See [trailmix-jump-gcd](../concepts/trailmix-jump-gcd.md)
   and [comparator-and-width-truncation](../concepts/comparator-and-width-truncation.md).
5. **Exchange rates from the note.** Measured from 2,000-nonce screens per
   point: depth 1 over a wide suffix returns about 4,200–4,300 CCX per unit of
   λ; depth 2 returns 1,700–3,500, best near divsteps 181–197. The old flat
   depth-2-from-133 cut ran at about 2,022 CCX per unit of λ.
6. **Re-mined strip, `src/point_add/deep_strip_keys.rs`.** A census of 858,012,672
   input pairs on the staircase stream at cap 1152 gives 8,908 dead and 2,057
   downgrade keys (10,965). The build logs "0 stale keys skipped". See
   [dead-gate-strip](../concepts/dead-gate-strip.md).
7. **Notes and nonce.** `src/point_add/memory/03-proven-floors.md` gains 66
   lines, and a new `memory/JOURNAL.md` is added (103 lines). Neither is
   compiled. `SUB4_TAIL_NONCE` default 2000000032904 → 77084001001127.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **Width narrowing: probabilistic.** An input whose `u`/`v` is still too large
  at a narrowed step loses a high bit when the top wire is reset. That gives a
  classical failure and usually phase garbage.
- The note says λ falls by 1.38 against the flat-128 cut of 3222da6, so the
  grind is about 4× cheaper. The clean-seed density, measured on the shipping
  stream, is P = 1.209e-8. The shipped seed was found in about 8.3e7 candidates.
- **Cap: exact** (layout only). **Strip: empirical**, as in [`a254a8a`](a254a8a.md).
- The note also records that the nonce cannot be screened by re-simulating only
  the tail. The Fiat–Shamir XOF absorbs every op before the run starts, so
  every shot must be re-simulated from op 0.
- The shipped nonce `77084001001127` draws a clean set: 0 classical, 0 phase and
  0 ancilla failures. See [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The circuit change is a new per-divstep width and window truncation profile.
That is comparator/width truncation. The cap is set to suit the new widths, and
the strip is a re-derivation.

5. **Run the benchmark.** Run `./setup.sh && ./benchmark.sh` with no `TLM_*` or

   - 1152 qubits

   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures
6. **Nonce caveat.** The score only reproduces with the nonce `77084001001127` and
   an identical op stream. Any other change needs a new nonce hunt and a strip
