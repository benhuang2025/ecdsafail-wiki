## Summary

The qubit peak of this route sits in the early steps of the dialog-GCD
forward walk (background in [3409fbd](3409fbd.md) and [99b9cff](99b9cff.md);
see [dialog-gcd-early](../concepts/dialog-gcd-early.md)). There the window of `u` and `v` is still
about 256 bits wide, and each step's controlled add/sub body asks for a
scratch pool of `2·body_len − 1` wires.

This commit adds a per-step notch list. Each listed step's body skips its top
`extra` bits. The shipped list is
`8:4, 9:4, 10:5, 11:3, 12:6, 13:6, 14:4, 15:4, 17:1`. Step 11 also keeps the
older 2-bit trio notch. Every notched bit removes two scratch wires from that
step, and the list was tuned so that every step in the region lands at or below
the new peak, 1297, nine qubits lower. It also saves 37 bit-steps × 8 = 296
Toffoli.

The prefilter used in the nonce hunt (`dialog_gcd_classical_filter.rs`) now
models these notches, and the step-11 trio notch, so that it can reject inputs
they break. The author did not build on `bde4caa`, the 1302-qubit frontier,
but on the older `1a7895c`.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

1. **`src/point_add/rounds/dialog/mod.rs`**
   - New `dialog_gcd_early_body_notch_extra_for_step(step)` parses
     `DIALOG_GCD_EARLY_BODY_NOTCHES` as `step:extra,…` and returns the largest
     `extra` listed for `step`, or 0.
   - `dialog_gcd_body_carry_trunc_width(active_width, step)` adds it to the trim
     `w`, after the band trim and the step-11 trio notch. The body width is
     `active_width − w`.
   - Only the body (gated load plus ripple) narrows. The comparator, the cswap
     and the shifts keep the full window.
2. **`src/point_add/mod.rs`, `configure_ecdsafail_submission_route()`**
   - New pin `DIALOG_GCD_EARLY_BODY_NOTCHES = "8:4,9:4,10:5,11:3,12:6,13:6,14:4,15:4,17:1"`.
   - `DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE`: 218004368140 → 1037913974. The comment for the
     old 2432 nonce is replaced by one that records this result.
3. **`src/point_add/dialog_gcd_classical_filter.rs`**
   - `DialogGcdFilterConfig` gains `trio_width_notch`, `trio_width_notch_step`,
     `trio_width_notch_extra` and `early_body_notches`, read from the same
     environment variables with the same defaults.
   - New `extra_body_notch_for_step` and `parse_step_extra_list`.
   - `body_carry_trunc_width` and `body_carry_trunc_width_fast` now subtract
     the extra notch, so the filter's `sub_low_window(v, u, body_w)` replays
     the same truncated body as the circuit on every step except 11. There
     `extra_body_notch_for_step` takes the larger of the trio notch (2) and the
     listed notch (3), so the filter trims 3 bits while the circuit, which adds
     them, trims 5.
   - Before this, the filter did not know about the step-11 notch at all.

**Per-step effect.** The steps around the peak and their trims after this
commit:

| step | active width | band trim | trio notch | new notch | total trim | `want` change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8, 9 | 256 | 0 | – | 4 | 4 | −8 |
| 10 | 256 | 0 | – | 5 | 5 | −10 |
| 11 | 256 | 0 | 2 | 3 | 5 | −6 (−10 vs. no notch) |
| 12, 13 | 254 | 0 | – | 6 | 6 | −12 |
| 14, 15 | 252 | 0 | – | 4 | 4 | −8 |
| 17 | 250 | 0 | – | 1 | 1 | −2 |

The widths come from `ceil((266 − 1.015·step)/2)·2`. Each step's peak is its
compressed-log and runway footprint plus its `want`. The footprint grows with
the step number, while the window only starts shrinking at step 12. That is
why the binders form a band around steps 8–17 instead of a single step. The
notches are sized to bring each of them down to about the same level. The
measured peak is 1297. See
[peak-qubits-and-budgets](../concepts/peak-qubits-and-budgets.md).

**Toffoli.** The new notches total 4+4+5+3+6+6+4+4+1 = 37 bit-steps. Each one
saves a gated-load CCX and a carry CCX in each of the 4 walks:
37 × 8 = 296, exactly the measured −296.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

Each notch is a width truncation. A notched step is exact only when the top
`trim` bits of `u` and `v` in that step's window, and the carry into them, are
zero. On steps 8–17 the operands have shrunk by only a few bits, so a 4- to
6-bit trim does fail on a small but real fraction of inputs. Those inputs give
a wrong GCD state, seen as a classical mismatch and as ancilla garbage after
the reverse walk. This raises λ noticeably.

Two things keep it shippable:
- The classical prefilter now replays the notched body, so nonces whose 9,024
  inputs hit a notch failure are rejected before the expensive check.
- Nonce `1037913974` passed the full evaluator: 0 classical, 0 phase and 0
  ancilla failures over all 9,024 shots.

See [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The mechanism is a per-step width truncation of the GCD body, so
comparator/width truncation matches the published label. Its purpose is the
9-qubit peak cut, not the 296 Toffoli. A peak-reduction or scratch-allocation
label would describe the outcome, as for `99b9cff`.

## Reproduce

1. **Start from the base.** Clone https://github.com/ecdsafail/ecdsafail-challenge
   and run `git checkout 1a7895c -- src/point_add`. The author built on
   `1a7895c`, not on the git parent `bde4caa` (zuiris). Diffing against the
   parent (232 lines) would wrongly show this commit reverting odinfree's
   `a88c9a1` (the measured `d` and `e`/`d` clears) and all of zuiris's
   `bde4caa` (its band-trim schedule, `DIALOG_GCD_FUSED_OVFCLEAR_MEASURED`, the
   `F_CUT4=189` move and the binder-notch code).
   `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol 1a7895c 94927be -- src/point_add` is 82 lines
   in 3 files.
2. **Apply the patch below** from the repo root with `git apply --ignore-whitespace`.
   The flag lets the patch apply whether the files use LF or CRLF line
   endings. It is the complete diff against the base.
3. **Check the tree.** `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol 94927be -- src/point_add`
   should print nothing.
4. **Run the benchmark.** Run `./setup.sh && ./benchmark.sh` with no
   `DIALOG_*` environment variables set, because `set_default_env` only fills
   in unset variables. Expect:
   - 1297 qubits
   - 1,460,157 average Toffoli (rounded)
   - score 1,893,823,629
   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures

The score only reproduces with `DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE=1037913974` and an identical
op stream. Any other change needs a new nonce hunt.
