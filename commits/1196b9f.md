## Summary

Before this commit, `src/point_add` held about 115k lines. Most of it was dead
code: the retired trailmix and dialog GCD routes, a 13.6k-line strip-key
table, a 16.8k-line action mask, backup copies and Python research scripts.
The live circuit was the ping-pong divider in `pingpong_div.rs` plus a few
helpers. This commit throws the whole tree away and rewrites only the live
circuit, as nine compact files totalling 4,511 lines. The algorithm is the same
one the frontier ran: the same 696-round width schedule, the same Karatsuba
square and the same tape-sign frees.

The score changes because of retuning on top of the rewrite. The three
truncation widths in the replay become per-round band tables keyed on the walk
width. The key move is the fold window: it gets narrower on the late rounds and
wider on the early ones. The fold in the first round of the trailing replay
batch is what set the peak, at `1210 + window`. With that window cut from 53
to 50, one qubit cap of 1260 replaces the frontier's 1263. Toffoli rises by 653
against the frontier, mostly because the multiply's windows were widened to buy
back λ. The score still drops by 1,887,408.

The rewrite has three conventions that later commits rely on:
- Every knob is a `set_default_env` pin in `build()` and is read through
  `required_env`, which panics if a pin is missing. The knobs are renamed from
  `SUB4_PP_*` to `PP_*`.
- The width schedule is the literal run-length string `PP_WIDTH_SCHEDULE`.
  Expanded, it is exactly the frontier's effective `value_width()`: all 696
  rounds match, which I checked by recomputing `c1adf3e`'s table, index
  rescale and +1 repairs.
- There is one qubit cap, `PP_WALK_MAX_QUBITS`. Every self-sizing ladder reads
  `cap − builder.active_qubits()`. The frontier instead had modelled budgets
  (`SUB4_PP_PEAK = SUB4_PP_WALK_PEAK = 1267`), which realised a peak of 1263.

### The tuning that moves the score (pins in `build()`, applied in `pingpong.rs`)

1. **Per-round fold window.** The frontier folded every replay cell at a flat
   53 bits in both directions (`SUB4_PP_REPLAY_FOLD_WINDOW = _MUL = 53`). Now
   the window is `PP_REPLAY_FOLD_WINDOW = 53` (divide) or
   `PP_REPLAY_FOLD_WINDOW_MUL = 54` (multiply), plus `fold_offset(round)`. That
   offset is:
   - `band_at(PP_FOLD_PROFILE = "38:0,32:-1,19:-3,0:-4", value_width(round))`,
   - plus 1 if `round < PP_FOLD_WIDEN = 242`.

   With the shipped schedule the windows (divide / multiply) are:

   | Rounds | Walk width | Window |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | 0..241 | ≥ 183 | 54 / 55 |
   | 242..615 | ≥ 38 | 53 / 54 |
   | 616..631 | 32..37 | 52 / 53 |
   | 632..662 | 19..31 | 50 / 51 |
   | 663..695 | < 19 | 49 / 50 |

   - The comment above `fold_shape()` gives the reason. Measured over 300,000
     shots per point, the rate at which the dropped fold carry matters is flat
     down to a walk width of about 45, then falls: 0.46 of the flat rate at
     width 35, 0.14 at 28, 0.07 at 21. Equalising the per-round error means the
     late rounds can take narrower windows. See
     [modular-fold](../concepts/modular-fold.md).
   - **This is the qubit saving.** The trailing replay batch (rounds above
     `PP_R2`) runs with the tape at full length and both walk registers loaned
     away (`loan_terminal`). Its first round's fold is the widest thing alive.
     The `PP_R2` comment puts the peak floor at `1210 + window` there. For the
     frontier's flat 53 that gives 1263, which matches its measured peak. Here the first trailing
     round is 649, at width 24, so 1210 + 50 = 1260.
   - **This is also most of the Toffoli rise.** The multiply's extra base bit
     and the `+1` on the first 242 rounds widen about 616 multiply folds and 242
     divide folds. The `mod.rs` comment prices the multiply bit alone at +688
     executed Toffoli, bought for −1.13 ± 0.31 λ.
2. **`PP_R2`: 645 → 648.** The comment gives the measured peak for each value:
   1262 for 615..630, 1260 for 631..655, 1261 for 656..657 and 1262 for 658 and
   up. The lower edge is where the trailing batch would start inside the 52-bit
   band. 648 sits mid-plateau.
3. **`r1` is computed, not pinned.** `head_boundary()` picks the largest round
   whose head-batch footprint still fits a two-chunk replay add under the cap.
   The comment says this gives 313 / 312 at 1260. The frontier pinned
   `SUB4_PP_R1 = 335` and `SUB4_PP_R1_MUL = 326`.
4. **Per-round chunk and flag compares.**
   - `chunk_compare(round) = 21 + band_at(PP_CHUNK_SHAPE = "38:0,25:-2,0:-4")`,
     so 21 / 19 / 17 bits.
   - `flag_compare(round) = 20 + band_at(PP_FLAG_SHAPE = "38:0,25:-2,0:-4")`,
     so 20 / 18 / 16 bits.
   - The frontier had a flat 21 and 20. Both are truncated compares inside
     `erase_with_compare`, so the late rounds save about one Toffoli per bit
     under a half-time condition. The measured tie rate at walk width below 25
     is about 0.04 of the wide rate.
   - `chunk_layout` still uses the flat 21 to place the chunk boundaries, so
     only the compare narrows. See
     [comparator-and-width-truncation](../concepts/comparator-and-width-truncation.md).
5. **Bit 0 parked for the whole walk** (`park_odd_bits` / `unpark_odd_bits`).
   - After round 0, `u[0]` and `v[0]` are constant ones. They are cleared with X
     and released right after round 0, and a fresh wire is set to 1 again at the
     matching walk-back.
   - The adders read bit 0 as the build-time flag `target0_is_one`.
   - The frontier only loaned these two wires around each walk add (its default
     path, which `SUB4_PP_LOW0_LOAN_OFF` switches off) and around interleaved
     replay rounds. See
     [venting-and-wire-loans](../concepts/venting-and-wire-loans.md).
6. **Two shared widths outside the replay.** Every constant-fold truncation reads
   `FOLD_GUARD = 21`, and every measured-erasure compare outside the replay reads
   `ERASE_COMPARE = 22`. This includes `round1_window = 31 + FOLD_GUARD`, the
   `f_slice` of `add_f_window` and the square's `window_add`. The comment
   derives the one-bit gap: compare gates sit under a condition and execute
   half the time.
7. **Kept as fixed code:** the tape-sign frees from
   [`5526766`](5526766.md) (`free_sign_bit`, `recompute_a0`,
   `recompute_bchain_sign`) and `PP_ROUNDS_MUL = 694`.
8. **Tail nonce:** `TAIL_NONCE = 2977985437`. The frontier's was
   `SUB4_PINGPONG_TAIL_NONCE = 3009322982643`.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **The rewrite itself** is meant to be exact. It is the same construction, and
  `mod.rs` asks for `md5sum ops.bin` as the acceptance test for refactors. But
  it is new code, so any slip would show up as a classical, phase or ancilla
  failure across many shots, not as a rare one.
- **Probabilistic, raises λ:**
  - the narrower late fold windows (classical failures, when a carry escapes
    the window);
  - the narrower late chunk and flag compares (phase failures, on a top-bit
    tie).
- **Probabilistic, lowers λ:** the wider multiply base window and the `+1` on
  early rounds.
- **Exact:** the single measured cap, bit-0 parking and the computed `r1`. They
  only change layout and wire lifetimes. Narrower ladders do bring more
  approximate chunk boundaries; the cap comment quotes about +0.06 λ for an
  earlier step of this kind.
- The shipped nonce draws a clean test set: 9,024 of 9,024 shots, with 0
  classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The score gain is the 3-qubit drop. It comes from narrowing the replay fold
window on the rounds that set the peak, as part of a per-round shaping of
fold-carry truncation. That makes it fold/carry truncation. Most of the line
count is deleted dead code, and the rewrite and measured cap are enablers, so
the family is Dead-Code and Redundancy Elimination. The runner-up was
comparator/width truncation, for the chunk and flag compare bands, but those
save Toffoli and do not move the peak.
