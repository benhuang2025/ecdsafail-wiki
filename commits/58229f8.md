## Summary

This commit changes one recipe value, `LF_MERGED_LATE_WIN`, from 58 to 52
bits. This is the fold window of Leapfrog's merged cell-and-barrel op on the
"late" ticks, where the standard 61-bit single-chunk fold does not fit under
the walk cap. A narrower window means fewer fold-ladder bits (and fewer or
smaller chunks) on those ops, for −734 Toffoli. The cost is a smaller escape
margin, so the change is a priced accuracy trade. The author reports
λ = 16.80 ± 0.25 failing shots per 9,024, against the 14.4 ± 0.4 that the
Leapfrog author reported for the parent line.

## What changed and why it saves Toffoli

**`src/point_add/leapfrog.rs`, `install_recipe()`:
`LF_MERGED_LATE_WIN` 58 → 52.**

How it is used (code unchanged since [`86221ad`](86221ad.md)):
- `merged_fits(active, t, rev)` first tries the standard merged op, whose
  window is `MW = LF_MERGED_WIN` (default 61). It takes that op when the fold
  ladder over bits `[4, MW)` fits in one chunk beside the op's fixed helpers.
- If that does not fit and `LF_MERGED_LATE=1`, it takes the late op with
  window `lw = LF_MERGED_LATE_WIN.min(MW)`, now 52. Its ladder over
  `[4, lw)` is split by `merged_plan` into chunks that fit the room.
  - The kept carries between chunks are erased exactly: an `hmr`, then a
    `[acc < add + cin]` phase compare on outcome 1.
  - Each non-final chunk of width `w` costs about `(w − 1)/2` expected extra
    Toffoli (`plan_x2`).
- So on each late op (forward and reverse) the ladder loses 6 carry
  positions, each one Toffoli. The fewer bits to place can also mean fewer
  split chunks.

The ticks on the standard path are untouched. So are the cells, the walk,
W1, the envelope and the cap.

**`src/point_add/mod.rs`**: `TAIL_NONCE` 1441041 → 140737643098289.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **The fold adds a small constant.** The merged fold adds `ov` and
  `(f − 1)(u − 15)` into the low `lw` bits of the payload. The `merged_win`
  comment says the constant is below 2^37, so at 61 bits there are "24 bits
  of escape margin". A carry or borrow that would leave the window is
  dropped, giving a wrong value that the **classical** channel catches.
- **The margin shrinks.** At 58 the late op had about 21 bits of margin; at
  52 it has about 15. My estimate of the miss rate per late op is of order
  `2^-(lw−37)`.
- **The author's λ.** 16.80 ± 0.25 failing shots per 9,024 (two cohorts of
  128 packets), under the 19.5 limit. In the author's later pricing
  ([`308a6ea`](308a6ea.md)), this trade cost +1.57 shots for −722 T, which is
  why that commit reverts it.
- The shipped nonce `140737643098289` draws a clean set: 0 classical,
  0 phase and 0 ancilla failures.

## Classification rationale

A modular-fold carry window is narrowed for fewer Toffoli at a higher
failure rate: fold/carry truncation.
