## Summary

The dialog-GCD inverse stores its per-step branch decisions in a compressed
quantum transcript, which stays live for most of the circuit. Before this
commit, the transcript used K2 pair blocks: two GCD steps (6 raw bits) packed
into 5 qubits. For 258 steps that is 129 blocks × 5 = 645 qubits.

This commit switches to K5 blocks. Five steps (15 raw bits) are packed into
12 qubits, so the transcript is 52 × 12 = 624 qubits, 21 fewer. The packer is
a 17-CCX reversible codec produced by Claude Fable-5, applied on top of two
of the old pair encoders.

The codec is more expensive: each block encode or decode costs 23 CCX for
five steps, against 7.5 for the pair code. The narrower transcript lets the
author lower the square's row cap by 8 bits (184 → 176) and the apply fold
window by one. Together these drop the peak from 1211 to 1203 qubits, for
+6,307 Toffoli.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

**Background: dialog-GCD transcript blocks (see [transcript-codecs](../concepts/transcript-codecs.md)
and [`420e0c2`](420e0c2.md) for the K2 pair layout).** Each K2 GCD step records
`b0`, `b0_and_b1` and `shift2`. Steps are grouped into blocks of
`dialog_gcd_sidecar_group_size()` steps.
- A block is decoded into a raw scratch block of
  `dialog_gcd_raw_block_len() = 3 × group_size` wires.
- The layout is `raw[2·slot]` = b0, `raw[2·slot+1]` = b0b1 and
  `raw[2·group + slot]` = s2.
- The block is consumed by the apply step and then re-encoded.

**1. K5 clean block (`src/point_add/rounds/dialog/compressed.rs` and
`config.rs`, new knob `DIALOG_GCD_K5_CLEAN_BLOCK=1`, needs `DIALOG_GCD_K2=1`).**
- `config.rs`:
  - `dialog_gcd_k5_clean_block_enabled()` makes `group_size = 5`,
    `dialog_gcd_block_bits() = 12` and raw block length = 15.
  - It switches **off** `dialog_gcd_k2_pair_compress_enabled()`, and it
    forces `dialog_gcd_compressed_block_lifecycle_enabled()` on.
  - `dialog_gcd_compressed_sidecar_blocks()` becomes ⌈258/5⌉ = 52: 51 full
    blocks and a final 3-step block.
- **Full-block encoder `dialog_gcd_k5_compress_raw_to_block`:**
  1. `emit_dialog_gcd_k5_pair_encoder` on steps 0–1 and on steps 2–3. This is
     the existing 3-CCX pair encoder. Each pair's five-bit core takes only 15
     values, so it compresses to four bits and frees one wire (`raw[0]` and
     `raw[4]` become 0).
  2. The remaining 13 information bits (`dialog_gcd_k5_data_from_raw`: four
     from each pair core, each pair's raw second `s2`, and step 4's three
     bits) go through `emit_dialog_gcd_k5_clean_compressor`.
     - This runs the gate list `DIALOG_GCD_K5_FABLE_GATES`: 17 CCX plus X/CX.
     - It uses one temporary wire (index 13), allocated and freed around the
       codec. Its 6 CCX build two three-control Toffolis, each a CCX
       into the temporary, one CCX that uses it, and the uncompute.
     - On the reachable support it maps data wire 10 to |0⟩.
  3. `dialog_gcd_k5_transfer_survivors` swaps the 12 surviving wires
     (`DIALOG_GCD_K5_DATA_WIRES = [0..9, 11, 12]`) into the block's 12
     transcript cells.
- **Decoder `dialog_gcd_k5_decompress_block_to_raw`:** the exact gate reverse.
- **Partial block (≤ 3 steps):** `dialog_gcd_k5_{compress,decompress}_partial_*`
  use the older round-763 6 → 5 compressor on the b0/b0b1 bits, plus the raw
  `s2` bits.
- **Wiring.** The four codec call sites dispatch to the K5 versions:
  `dialog_gcd_copy_compressed_block_to_raw`, `dialog_gcd_clear_raw_block_copy`,
  the forward-walk block compress in `…_tobitvector_steps_block_lifecycle`,
  and the reverse-walk decompress.

Per five steps, the K5 code stores 12 qubits where the pair code stored 12.5.
Over the walk that is 645 → 624 transcript qubits. The raw scratch block grows
from 6 to 15 wires, but it is live only around one block at a time.

**Side effects on earlier levers.** Because pair compress is now off:
- The in-place K2 decode of [`420e0c2`](420e0c2.md)
  (`DIALOG_GCD_K2_APPLY_INPLACE_RAW_BLOCK`, still set to 1) is inert. The
  apply goes back to swapping the block into `raw_block`, and with
  `DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_REPLAY_SWAP_HOST=1` the emptied 12 transcript cells
  become the add/sub's `clean_scratch`.
- So the special fold of [`461a4a3`](461a4a3.md) borrows 11 carry wires
  (`inner_scratch` = 12 − 1) where it borrowed 4.

**2. Knobs (`src/point_add/mod.rs`, `configure_ecdsafail_submission_route()`).**
- `DIALOG_GCD_K5_CLEAN_BLOCK` = `1` (new).
- `SQUARE_ROW_MAX_SEG`: 184 → 176.
  - Rows of width 177 to 184 (`i = 73..80`) are now windowed, so the widest
    one-piece row register drops from 184 to 176 bits. That lowers the
    square's transient peak by 8.
  - It adds 8 rows × 2 (forward and inverse) measured boundary cleanups (see
    [`d636d62`](d636d62.md)).
- `DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_CARRY_TRUNC_W`: 18 → 17. This is the apply's fused
  `double_y` / `halve_y` fold window, read by `fold_only_carry_trunc_window()`.
  It lowers that fold's high-water by one and drops one carry position.
- Both `DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE` lines: 30000009780549 → 9600076011007. Only the
  first line takes effect.

`DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_CLEAN_COMPARE_BITS = 19` and
`DIALOG_GCD_WIDTH_SLOPE_X1000 = 1015` are unchanged from the base.

**Cost.** The K5 codec costs 2 × 3 + 17 = 23 CCX per full block per encode or
decode, against 3 CCX per pair, about 7.5 per five steps. The codec runs on
every forward and reverse walk block and on every apply decode/re-encode,
in both GCDs. Most of the +6,307 is this codec overhead and the 16 new square
cleanups. I did not split it further.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **The codec is exact on the reachable transcript language.**
  - The pair encoders and the Fable codec are reversible gate lists. The
    decoder is the exact reverse, and the temporary wire 13 is computed and
    uncomputed coherently.
  - The compression relies on the GCD never producing the unreachable bit
    patterns. Those are what map wire 10 (and the pair cores' zero wires) to
    0. If an unreachable pattern ever appeared, a surviving cell would hold
    information that the swap-out treats as zero. That would leave a dirty
    raw wire (an ancilla failure) or a wrong transcript (a classical failure).
  - The submission note says the codec was verified.
- **The square cap and the fold window are the usual levers.** The cap is
  value-exact but adds sixteen 21-bit truncated cleanups, where a tie gives a
  phase failure. The fold window is probabilistic at about 2^-17 per call, and
  a dropped carry gives a classical failure.
- The shipped nonce `9600076011007` draws a clean set (9,024 shots, 0 / 0 / 0).
  See [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The new mechanism is a denser reversible code for the GCD transcript. That is
Codec / Encoding. The square cap and fold window are the dials that turn the
freed transcript width into a lower global peak.
