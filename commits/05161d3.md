## Summary

q71a takes dysnasia's own q73d circuit from 1173 to 1171 qubits by lowering
the walk cap `HEO_PIN_PP_WALK_MAX_QUBITS` two steps. With two fewer wires of
room, the chunked adds sized under the cap cost more. The commit pays for it
in five ways:

- **Exact Toffoli savings in the tape codec.** It ports jackylee0424's
  shared pair carry from [`9db31da`](9db31da.md) (`EXACT_PAIR_CARRY`). It
  adds a reverse twin (`X2_REV_PAIR`) and applies the forward version to two
  division ticks (`X2_DIVFWD_PAIR_T`).
- **Fewer live wires.** The division batch keeps one fewer tape group raw,
  and the multiply leg's head batch starts five ticks later.
- **A new per-cell bridge/room table** (226 cells), re-fitted for the 1171
  cap.
- **λ trades.** Narrower walk envelopes on 14 ticks are a sell. A wider fold
  (`FOLD_WIDEN` 112 → 128) and 99 per-cell compare bits are buys, and 22
  cells are sold.
- **377 rewrite rows instead of 105.** The author says 59 of them are not
  exact (each failed once in 16M census shots).

The net is +1,455 Toffoli at two qubits fewer, which lowers the score by
185,327 against q73d. The author reports λ = 19.333 ± 0.032.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

### 1. Recipe restructure (`mod.rs`)

`install_skywalk_submission_recipe()` now has two loops.

- **The first loop** holds the old recipe. In it, the values that sky9–q73d
  had edited in place are set back to their sky8 values: `HEO_FIT_K` 23,
  cap 1174, `FOLD_GUARD` 21, `FOLD_WIDEN` 68, `ERASE_COMPARE` 25,
  `TERMINAL_FW` 50, and so on. The sky9–q73d comment blocks are deleted. The
  first loop also skips any name for which `SKY_SKIP_<name>` is set. That
  hook is inert, because `build()` clears the environment first.
- **The second loop**, the "q71a package", sets its values over the first,
  last one wins.

The effective changes against q73d (`e3fd358`) are only these. Everything
else in the second loop restates q73d's value.

| knob | q73d | q71a |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `HEO_PIN_PP_WALK_MAX_QUBITS` | 1173 | **1171** |
| `HEO_R1M` | 99 | 94 |
| `HEO_DIV_LIFO_N` | 2 | 1 |
| `HEO_PIN_PP_FOLD_WIDEN` | 112 | 128 |
| `GO_CELLB` | 4 entries | 4 + 53 entries (below) |
| `EXACT_PAIR_CARRY`, `X2_REV_PAIR`, `X2_DIVFWD_PAIR_T` | unset | `1`, `1`, `384,389` |
| `SKYX_K3B_RETUNE` | path to `q73a_k3b_retune.txt` | `"1"` (only checked for presence) |
| `TAIL_NONCE` | 25700399837515 | 28000421609163 |

### 2. Walk cap 1171

- Each payload cell's chunked add, and each windowed fit add, is laid out
  under `walk_max_qubits() − active_qubits()`.
- Two fewer wires means more chunk boundaries, each with a measured carry
  erase, or more in-place bridge stages. Both cost Toffoli.
- The peak follows the cap. See
  [peak-qubits-and-budgets](../concepts/peak-qubits-and-budgets.md).

### 3. Shared pair carries (`heo_carry.rs`), three exact levers

- **`EXACT_PAIR_CARRY`** is jackylee0424's mechanism, ported (the doc
  comment says "port of 9db31da9 EXACT_PAIR_CARRY").
  - On the multiply forward pass, the tick at letter u % 5 == 3 keeps its
    first carry, and the tick at u % 5 == 4 turns it into a shared product.
    That product goes into `Tape.outer_shared[g]`.
  - `pack5` then uses `pair_pack_shared`, which costs 1 CCX, instead of
    `pair_pack`, which costs CCX + Fredkin.
  - The measured product's phase is repaired in `pair_unpack` from the
    stored `Group.m_pair`.
  - [`9db31da`](9db31da.md) describes the gates.
- **`X2_REV_PAIR`** is new here. It adds `pair_unpack_retained`, documented
  as an "exact inverse of pair_pack_shared on the packed pair … that also
  returns the shared product p = anc*(1-b) = h4*typ5 … 2 CCX like
  pair_unpack".
  - In a sharing reverse tick (`divrev`, or `mulrev` / `mulrev2` with
    `GO_MULREV_SHARE` / `GO_MULREV2_SHARE`, both on), `repack`'s pair unpack
    of group g uses this function. The tick must be t4 or t4 + 1 (t4 = the
    group's letter 4), and on `mulrev` t4 ≥ `HEO_R2M`.
  - The returned product goes to `outer_shared[g]`.
  - The reverse tick at u % 5 == 4 takes it as its `codec` input, as the
    existing u % 5 == 2 share does.
  - The submission note says this lets "the next reverse carry tick skip one
    CCX".
- **`X2_DIVFWD_PAIR_T=384,389`** turns on the forward pair share for
  `divfwd` at ticks 384 and 389. At those ticks, u % 5 == 3 also sets
  `keep_low`, and the u % 5 == 4 product goes to `outer_shared`.

All three are exact. The author measured exactly +0.0000 λ on 400 paired
nonces. See [transcript-codecs](../concepts/transcript-codecs.md) and
[measurement-uncompute](../concepts/measurement-uncompute.md).

### 4. Fewer live wires in the batches

- **`HEO_DIV_LIFO_N` 2 → 1.**
  - The code comment reads "HEO_DIV_LIFO_N = n leaves the last n+1 groups
    raw". So the division batch now keeps 2 groups unpacked instead of 3.
  - A raw group holds 10 wires and a packed one 8, so this saves 2 wires
    while that group is held. It costs one more pack/unpack.
- **`HEO_R1M` 99 → 94.** The multiply leg's head batch now starts at tick 93
  instead of 98. Ticks 94–98 move into the fused walk-back. sky16
  ([`799153a`](799153a.md)) explains the mechanism: a shorter head batch
  runs with a shorter tape alive.

### 5. Per-cell bridge/room table: `q71a_k3b_retune.txt` (226 rows)

- The `include_str!` in `k3b_cell` switches from `q73a_k3b_retune.txt` to
  this file. It has the same `idx bridge room tick dir` format: 121 division
  and 105 multiply rows.
- Bridge deltas: 166 × +0, 33 × +1, 5 × +2, 2 × +3, 20 × −1. Room deltas
  run from −4 to +4.
- The room assert is widened to ±16 "as in 9db31da9". This table does not
  need the wider range.

### 6. Walk envelopes: `sky8_env_*_iA.*.100.txt` → `q71a_env_div.txt` / `q71a_env_mul.txt`

- `heo::config()` and `config_mul()` now `include_str!` the new files. The
  comment calls them "the q72 DMX one".
- The tick counts are unchanged: 393 division and 392 multiply.
- Against sky8, the division envelope is narrower on 10 ticks (254–258, 268,
  301, 304, 355, 357): −7 swap-width bits and −6 add-width bits in total.
  The multiply envelope is narrower on 4 ticks (210, 254–256): −2 and −3.
- Each tick's rail cost is `(esw − 1) + (ead − 1)` CCX, so this saves about
  13 CCX per division rail traversal and 5 per multiply rail traversal. It
  is an envelope sell: a walk that needs the dropped bit at those ticks now
  fails.

### 7. Per-cell compare bands: `GO_CELLB`

The q73d list (`div:150-155:-1,div:220-329:-1,mul:105-194:1,mul:25-49:1`)
gains:

- `mul:369-390:-1`: sell, 22 multiply cells;
- `mul:57-104:1`: buy, 48 multiply cells;
- 51 single-tick `+1` buys, 25 on division cells and 26 on multiply cells,
  at ticks 1–94 and 269–378.

`go_cell_delta` sums all matching entries, and dB widens each cell's
chunk-boundary erase. The author calls this the "p4 per-cell compare
re-balance". Together with `FOLD_WIDEN` 112 → 128, which gives one more
fold bit to cells at proxy rounds 112–127, it buys back λ for the other
sells. See [comparator-and-width-truncation](../concepts/comparator-and-width-truncation.md)
and [modular-fold](../concepts/modular-fold.md).

### 8. Rewrite rows: `q73d_rewrite.txt` (105) → `q71a_rewrite.txt` (377)

- The loader is unchanged: one global shift, then CCX → CX chain. See
  [dead-gate-strip](../concepts/dead-gate-strip.md).
- The rows sit throughout the stream: 188 before op 4.5M, 52 in
  4.5M–5.3M and 137 after.
- The code comment says "SAT-proved and census rows measured at 0 fails".
  The submission note is more specific:
  - 318 rows are SAT-proved or measured at 0 fails in 16M shots per CCX;
  - 59 rows "are census rows at 1 fail in 16M", kept where they saved
    2,500–5,200 T per unit of λ.
- So those 59 rows are a small error sell.
- 272 more rows than q73d remove about 272 more CCX per shot.

### 9. Housekeeping

- The eleven unused rewrite tables (`sky9` … `sky21`, `q73d`) are deleted.
- `q73a_k3b_retune.txt` is deleted.
- Two of jackylee0424's `3161bd2` tables, `cap1173_bridge_room_profile_v2.txt`
  and `q1173_exact_rows.txt`, are added unchanged. Nothing reads them; only
  a comment names the first.
- `cell_div` / `cell_mul` read an optional `TERMINAL_FW_DIV` /
  `TERMINAL_FW_MUL` before `TERMINAL_FW`. Neither is set, so the terminal
  fold window stays 40.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **Exact parts.**
  - The three pair-carry levers: the codec output is the same and the
    phases are repaired. A bug would show up as **phase** or **ancilla**
    failures on many shots.
  - The cap, the LIFO depth, `HEO_R1M` and the bridge/room table: these
    change layout and scratch only.
  - The 318 proved or 0-fail rewrite rows.
- **Probabilistic parts.**
  - The 14 narrower envelope ticks. A walk wider than the envelope leaves
    garbage on the rails, which shows up as a **classical** or **ancilla**
    failure.
  - The 22 sold multiply cells and the 59 census rows, which give **phase**
    or **classical** failures.
  - The buys (`FOLD_WIDEN` 128 and 99 `GO_CELLB` bits) offset them.
- **λ.** The submission note gives 19.333 ± 0.032 on 16,384 nonces (est + 2 SE
  19.397), below the 19.5 limit of the 8ee337c note and above PR 419's 18.
- The shipped nonce `28000421609163` draws a clean island: 0 classical,
  0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The headline is two qubits fewer from a lower cap, with live-wire changes
(`HEO_DIV_LIFO_N`, `HEO_R1M`, per-cell room) that make it fit. That is
ancilla-lifetime / venting. The Toffoli side is a mix of exact savings
(pair carries, rewrite rows) and λ re-balancing. None of these is the reason
for the commit.
