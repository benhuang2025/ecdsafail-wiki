## Summary

On the 1382-qubit tree, the peak sat in the GCD apply pass. It was in the
modular add and subtract that run once per GCD step, and the next phase down
was at 1320. These operations add a 256-bit operand in chunks. Two transients
set the peak:

- the carry lane of the last chunk's fast ripple, 86 wires wide (1382);
- the carry lane of the comparator that clears the chunk-boundary carries,
  171 wires wide (1381).

This commit removes both. The last chunk now uses the textbook Cuccaro
MAJ/UMA adder, which needs no carry lane but costs 2 Toffoli per bit instead of
1. The operand is cut into five chunks (four of 50 bits and a final 57) instead
of four (three of about 57 and a final 87).
The boundary comparator is split at bit 100 into two 100-bit halves. The high
half takes its carry-in from the boundary carry already stored at bit 100, so
the split costs no extra gates. Every apply transient then fits under 1320, and
the peak falls to the GCD body's own floor: 1382 → 1320 qubits.

The price is about +86 Toffoli per apply add or subtract, about +45,000 in
total. The author also runs the GCD for 260 steps instead of 258, which costs
about 6,000 more Toffoli but removed the residual failures. The net is
+51,196 Toffoli. That is still a 1.3% better score. At 1320 qubits, break-even
for dropping 62 qubits is 62 × 1,514,221 / 1320 ≈ 71,000 extra Toffoli.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

**Background.** The circuit is the dialog-GCD route (`emit_dialog_gcd_raw_pa`
in `src/point_add/mod.rs`). Two calls each run a reversible binary GCD: the
quotient dy/dx and the product with c = ox − Rx. Each call's apply pass does,
per GCD step, `y = 2y mod p`, then `y = y ± b0·x mod p`, then a conditional
swap. [98416e4](98416e4.md) has the GCD step, and [af0f90a](af0f90a.md) has the
apply step. The modular add is
`dialog_gcd_cmod_add_materialized_pseudomersenne_chunked`. It does a raw
controlled add `acc_ext += ctrl·source` on 257 bits with
`dialog_gcd_add_ctrl_chunked_low_to_ext`, then the fold and an overflow clean.
The subtract path, `dialog_gcd_sub_ctrl_chunked_low_to_ext`, mirrors it.

**How the chunked add works** (`dialog_gcd_add_ctrl_chunked_low_to_ext`). The
chunk upper bounds come from `dialog_gcd_chunk_hi`. For each non-final chunk
`[lo, hi)`:

```
f    = ctrl & source[lo..hi]                     # gated copy, hi−lo fresh wires, 1 CCX per bit
cuccaro_add_fast(f ++ zero, acc[lo..hi] ++ cout, carry)   # 1 Toffoli per bit, needs a hi−lo carry lane
clear f by hmr + cz_if                           # free
carry = cout                                     # the boundary carry is kept, not erased
```

The final chunk adds into the top of `acc_ext` without a `cout`. Then one
comparator, `ccx_cmp_lt_into_fast_prefix_targets(acc[..p], source[..p], ctrl, targets)`,
recomputes the carry at every boundary and XORs it into the stored `cout`s,
clearing them all. Here `p` is the last boundary. This works because the carry
out of bit p of a sum equals `[sum mod 2^p < source mod 2^p]`. The comparator
needs `c_in` plus `p` carry wires, which it erases by measurement. See
[adders-and-carries](../concepts/adders-and-carries.md) for the two adder styles.

**The changes**, all in `src/point_add/mod.rs`:

1. **Chunk layout.**
   - `DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_CHUNKED_F_BLOCKS` goes from 4 to 5.
   - `…_CUSTOM4` goes from 1 to 0, and the new `…_CUSTOM5` is 1.
   - The cuts go from 58/114/170 to 50/100/150/200, with the new
     `DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_CHUNKED_F_CUT4` = 200.
   - `dialog_gcd_chunk_hi` gains a `blocks == 5` branch that reads the four cuts
     and asserts that they increase.
   - Non-final chunks drop to 50 bits, so their `f` plus carry lane is smaller.
2. **`DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_FINAL_LOWQ=1`** (new knob). In the final chunk of both the
   add and the subtract, it replaces
   `cuccaro_add_fast_low_to_ext(f, acc_ext[lo..hi], carry)` (1 Toffoli per bit,
   with a fresh `n`-wire carry lane) with `cuccaro_add(f ++ zero, acc_ext[lo..hi], carry)`.
   - `cuccaro_add` is a MAJ/UMA ripple: about 2 Toffoli per bit, one extra zero
     wire, no carry lane. `cuccaro_sub` is its inverse.
   - The final chunk is now `[200, 257)`, so this removes a 56-wire lane.
3. **`DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_BOUNDARY_SPLIT=100`** (new knob). The boundary-clear
   comparator now runs `ccx_cmp_lt_into_fast_prefix_targets_split` instead of
   one 200-bit sweep. A new helper pair,
   `cmp_lt_fast_prefix_window_forward` and `cmp_lt_fast_prefix_window_inverse`,
   runs one window of the comparator with a given carry-in.
   - **Hosted case**, which is the shipped one, because the split equals the
     cut at 100. The window `[100, 200)` runs first, with the stored boundary
     carry at bit 100 as its carry-in. It clears the boundary carries at 150 and
     200 and unwinds. Then the window `[0, 100)` runs with a fresh carry-in and
     clears the carries at 50 and 100. Each window needs only 100 carry wires,
     and there is no recompute.
   - **Generic case**, used when the split is not on a cut. It materialises the
     carry at the split into a fresh `boundary` qubit and recomputes the low
     window to clear it afterwards. That costs one more qubit and one more
     low-window pass.
4. **`DIALOG_GCD_ACTIVE_ITERATIONS`: 258 → 260.** This is two more GCD steps.
   - The transcript grows from 86 to 87 blocks.
   - The author's trace shows the peak staying at 1320 for 260 and 261 steps,
     and rising to 1327 at 262.
   - The comment says the two steps "remove the residual phase failures at the
     custom-five seed".
5. **`DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE`: 3577 → 108.**
6. **No-op edits.**
   - `set_phase` labels inside the chunked add and subtract
     (`dialog_gcd_apply_chunk_{add,sub}_{load,ripple,clear,final_*,boundary_clear}`).
     These emit no ops and only feed the phase-peak trace.
   - About 20 rustfmt-only reformatting hunks elsewhere in the file.
7. **New file `src/point_add/memory/2026-06-05-1320-apply-teardown.md`** (115
   lines). The author's log of the per-phase peaks before the change:
   - final ripple 1382, boundary clear 1381, other chunk ripples 1326;
   - `dialog_gcd_compressed_block_tobitvector_*` 1320;
   - the ROUND84 square 1309.
   It also records the intermediate seeds (split 53, cuts 54/108/162, 258 steps
   with 8 + 4 failures) and the final configuration.

**Toffoli cost.** These are rough counts per apply add or subtract.

- **Chunks.** Before, 256 bits rippled at 1 Toffoli each. Now 200 bits ripple at
  1 Toffoli and 56 at 2, which is +56.
- **Comparator.** The boundary comparator grows from 170 to 200 bits, which is
  +30.
- **Total per call.** That is about +86 per call. There are 260 apply steps in
  each of two GCD calls, which gives about +45,000.
- **Extra steps.** The two extra GCD steps add two apply steps per call, each
  about 1,400 Toffoli on 256-bit registers, plus a little GCD body: about
  +6,000.
- **Measured.** The total is +51,196. The author's note measures the
  final-chunk swap alone at about +44,000 on an earlier cut layout.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **The apply changes are exact.**
  - `cuccaro_add` and `cuccaro_add_fast_low_to_ext` compute the same sum.
  - The split comparator computes the same carries as the single sweep. When
    `ctrl` = 1, the stored carry at bit 100 is exactly the comparator's carry
    into bit 100. When `ctrl` = 0, the targets are not touched, and the window
    unwinds with the same carry-in, so it leaves no garbage.
  - The new chunk cuts only move where carries are kept, and the boundary sweep
    clears all of them exactly.
  - A bug here would show up as ancilla or phase garbage, not a wrong value.
- **The only change in approximation is the step count**, which goes up. So
  convergence failures can only get rarer.
- **The truncations themselves are unchanged**: slope 1005, margin 10,
  comparator 52, fold and double windows 23. But the new op stream draws a new
  test set. With 258 steps and the teardown, the author saw 8 classical and
  4 phase failures at the inherited nonce. With 260 steps the inherited nonce
  left 2 classical failures. A sweep then found `108`, which draws a clean set:
  0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures over all 9,024 shots.

## Classification rationale

The qubit saving comes from shortening scratch lanes that are live at the peak:
the final chunk's carry lane and half of the boundary comparator's carry lane.
Each transient is restructured so it needs fewer wires at once. That fits
Register and Scratch Allocation → ancilla-lifetime / venting. A close runner-up
would be an adder swap, since the final chunk trades a 1-Toffoli carry-lane adder
for a 2-Toffoli in-place one. But the motive and the effect are the peak, not
the gate count.
