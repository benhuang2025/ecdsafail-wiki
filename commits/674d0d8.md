## Summary

This commit continues [`cf310ec`](cf310ec.md). It takes 15 more qubits off
the peak and pays +16,412 Toffoli. At this size a qubit is worth about
1,200 Toffoli, so the trade wins by 2.08 M of score. There are four groups of
changes:

1. **Tail codec.** The GCD walk has 258 steps, so its last K5 block holds only
   3 steps. Before this commit that block took a full 12-qubit block, of which
   8 cells were used. Now a 5-qubit code stores it, one of 32 supported
   three-step patterns. The apply decodes it one step at a time, so its
   15-wire raw block is mostly freed.
2. **Fold wires.** The fused `double_y` / `halve_y` fold now parks 15 low
   carries instead of 7. It also hosts its four derived control wires in
   parked carry slots. The special add/sub fold parks 13 carries instead of 5.
3. **Per-step repairs.** At 36 apply steps the fused-fold window is widened
   from 18 to 19 or 20 bits, and at 14 steps the special-fold window is
   widened the same way. The park count rises by the same amount there, so
   the peak stays flat. At 7 GCD steps the branch compare is widened from 46
   to 48 bits. The author found these sites empirically, as the places where
   hard inputs failed.
4. **Co-binder dials.**
   - The square row cap goes from 158 to 143.
   - The apply ripple uses 20 chunks at 19 hand-placed cuts and borrows carry lanes from
     unused boundary cells.
   - The transcript runway that lives in the dead high bits of `u` may now
     start in the middle of a block.

**1. Tail-3 "top32" codec (`rounds/dialog/compressed.rs`).**
- 258 = 51 × 5 + 3, so the last block has 3 steps, which is 9 raw bits
  (b0, b0b1 and s2 per step).
- `dialog_gcd_compressed_sidecar_block_bits(last)` becomes 5
  (`DIALOG_GCD_K5_TAIL3_DATA_WIRES.len()`). Before, the partial block occupied
  a full 12-cell block (`dialog_gcd_block_bits()`) and used 8 cells. So the
  transcript register shrinks by 7 qubits for its whole lifetime.
- **The code.** `DIALOG_GCD_K5_TAIL3_TOP32_SUPPORT` lists 32 nine-bit
  patterns.
  - The encoder `dialog_gcd_k5_tail3_top32_toggle_code_from_raw` writes the
    5-bit code. Each code bit is a fixed constant (`…_CODE_CONSTANT = 29`)
    XOR an algebraic normal form of degree ≤ 2 in the raw bits
    (`…_ENCODER_ANF`).
  - The decoder `…_toggle_raw_from_code` clears the raw bits with a degree
    ≤ 5 ANF of the code (`…_DECODER_ANF`).
  - Multi-control terms use `dialog_gcd_toggle_mcx_with_dirty`, a
    dirty-ancilla CCX ladder that borrows idle raw-block wires.
  - The self-test `dialog_gcd_k5_tail3_top32_codec_selftest` checks three
    things: the 32 codes are distinct, decode(encode) is the identity, and
    the circuit is phase- and garbage-clean on the support.
- **Stream apply (`…_TAIL3_TOP32_STREAM_APPLY=1`).** In both apply
  lifecycles the tail block is not decoded into the full 15-wire raw block.
  Instead:
  - Ten raw wires (`…_stream_dynamic`) are freed for the whole block, and 5
    (`…_STREAM_SCRATCH_WIRES = [6,7,8,9,13]`) stay as the add/sub's clean
    scratch.
  - For each step, the code reacquires that step's three raw wires, decodes
    only them from the code (`…_toggle_slot_raw_from_code`), runs the
    double/halve, add/sub and swap, then re-toggles and frees them.
  - The code stays in place throughout.
- `dialog_gcd_classical_filter.rs` adds `Tail3Top32Mismatch`. Any input whose
  last three transcript steps are not in the list is treated as a hard input.

**2. Runway partial block (`DIALOG_GCD_RUNWAY_PARTIAL_BLOCK=1`,
`dialog_gcd_runway_layout`).**
- The existing `…_COMPRESSED_LOG_U_HIGH_RUNWAY` places the transcript cells
  of late blocks on high bits of `u` that the GCD no longer uses.
- The layout search used to start hosting only at a block boundary. Now it may
  start at any slot inside the first hosted block (`first_slot`). So more
  transcript cells live on dead `u` bits instead of their own qubits.

**3. Fold parking and control hosting (`arith/const_arith.rs`, `…_STEP_MAP` readers).**
- **Fused fold.** `fold_ripple_freed_tail_ed` reads
  `fold_park_low_carries_at_step(step)`. With 15 or more parked carries and
  `DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_HOST_DERIVED_CONTROLS=1`, it takes the `host_all_derived`
  path.
  - The four derived controls `h`, `xed`, `eord` and `n10` are rebuilt into
    parked low-carry slots that are known zero. Their own wires are released
    before the 34-wire low-carry lane is allocated.
  - The code comment calls this "value/phase exact" and says it "removes four
    qubits from the fused-fold high-water".
  - Parking 15 instead of 7 removes 8 more low carries from the tail
    high-water. The extra recompute work is the post-sum carry formula of
    [`cf3c6f1`](cf3c6f1.md), now on positions 7 to 14.
- **Special fold.** `cadd/csub_nbit_const_direct_trunc_fast_releasing_scratch_at_step`
  reads `special_fold_park_low_carries_at_step(step)`, which is 13 by
  default. The parked fold of [`cf310ec`](cf310ec.md) therefore holds 8 fewer
  wires.
- **Per-step windows.**
  - `dialog_gcd_fused_fold_carry_trunc_window(step)` and
    `dialog_gcd_special_fold_carry_trunc_window(step)` read the step maps
    above.
  - At the listed steps the window grows by 1 or 2 bits. That lowers the
    chance that a carry escapes at those steps.
  - The matching `…_PARK_LOW_CARRIES_STEP_MAP` entries park 1 or 2 more
    carries there. The longer tail is offset by fewer live low carries, so
    the peak does not move.

**4. Branch compare repairs (`rounds/dialog/config.rs`).**
`dialog_gcd_compare_bits_for_step` first checks the new
`DIALOG_GCD_COMPARE_STEP_BITS` map. Seven late GCD steps use a 48-bit branch
compare instead of 46.

**5. Apply chunks and smaller edits (`rounds/dialog/mod.rs`).**
- **Chunks.** Twenty chunks with explicit cuts. Their widths shrink from 17
  bits at the bottom to 9 or 10 at the top, so the widest transient
  `ctrl & a` chunk is narrower where the accumulator is widest.
- **Borrowed carry lanes.** With `…_BORROW_FUTURE_BOUNDARY_CARRIES=1`, each
  chunk's ripple borrows its carry lane from boundary-host cells that later
  chunks have not used yet.
- **Hosted flag compares.** The overflow and underflow flag-clean compares now
  go through `dialog_gcd_cmp_lt_phase_conditioned_hosted`. It borrows `c_in`
  and the carry lane from `clean_scratch`, but only when the scratch has at
  least `k + 1` wires, because `DIALOG_GCD_PARTIAL_HOST_COMPARATOR` is not set.
  With the 11 to 12 cells of a K5 block against `k` = 20 or more, I believe
  this falls back to allocation in most calls. I did not trace it.
- **`DIALOG_GCD_K5_RELEASE_SCALE_BITS = 5`** splits the shift-time block
  release into two `free_vec` calls. With `…_FREE_CLEAN_BLOCK_DURING_SHIFT`
  already on, I found no net change in live wires.
- **`DIALOG_GCD_K5_PARTIAL_RAW_RELEASE = 6`** is read only on the non-streamed
  partial-block path. The only partial block is streamed, so it appears
  inert.

**6. Square row cap.** `SQUARE_ROW_MAX_SEG` 158 → 143. Rows of width 144 to
158 (`i = 99..113`) are now windowed. The square's largest transient row drops
by 15 bits, at the cost of 30 more measured boundary cleanups, each about 30
Toffoli.

**Also in the diff, not in the circuit:**
- A notes file, `src/point_add/memory/2026-06-13-q1192-wmi-cuda-search.md`,
  with the author's GPU campaign log.
- A second tail-3 codec (`…_TAIL3_FIXED_LAST`, a pair code that assumes the
  last step's digit is 4). It is set to 0.
- The tail-3 filter checks in the classical prefilter.

**Cost.** The +16,412 Toffoli is the sum of several parts:
- more parked-carry recomputes in both folds, at every apply step;
- the control re-derivation in the hosted fused fold;
- about 900 for the 30 new square cleanups;
- the widened windows and compares at the repair sites;
- the ANF decode/encode of the tail block.

I did not measure the split.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **Exact parts.**
  - Parking and hosting: each parked or hosted wire is recomputed from
    unchanged inputs before use.
  - Streamed decoding: each slot is decoded and re-encoded around its use.
  - The runway re-layout, the chunk cuts and the borrowed lanes: these are
    the same arithmetic on different wires.
  - The square cap, which is value-exact.
- **The tail codec is exact only on its 32 supported patterns.**
  - The submission note calls this the reachable support of the last three
    steps. Those steps come after the GCD has essentially converged, so few
    patterns occur.
  - The constant's name ("TOP32") suggests the list may be the 32 most common
    patterns instead. I did not enumerate it.
  - Either way, the classical filter rejects any input outside the list. An
    unsupported pattern would decode wrongly, which gives classical or
    ancilla failures.
- **Probabilistic parts.**
  - The fold windows still drop carries that run more than 18 (or 19, 20)
    bits past the constant, which gives a classical failure.
  - The 46/48-bit branch compares can misjudge a branch, which gives a
    classical failure.
  - The square cleanups use truncated compares, where a tie gives a phase
    failure.
  - The per-step repairs widen exactly the sites where the author's
    hard-input search saw failures. That lowers λ without paying the width
    everywhere.
- The shipped nonce `11156415` draws a clean set: 9,024 shots, 0 classical,
  0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. The author found it with a CUDA search,
  confirmed it with an independent Rust replay, and then ran the trusted
  evaluator. See [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The census files this under the K5 codec, for the new tail-3 code. That code
is worth 7 of the 15 qubits. The rest comes from lifetime cuts (fold parking,
control hosting, streamed decode, the runway) and the square cap. The label
fits the largest single lever. **Register and Scratch Allocation →
ancilla-lifetime / venting** would be the runner-up.

+# q1192 WMI CUDA search
+
+## Candidate
+
+The current 1192-qubit candidate uses:
+
+```text
+DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_CARRY_TRUNC_W=18
+DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_PARK_LOW_CARRIES=13
+DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_HOST_N10=1
+SQUARE_ROW_MAX_SEG=165
+KAL_FOLD_CARRY_TRUNC_W=20
+DIALOG_GCD_SPECIAL_FOLD_RELEASE_SCRATCH=1
+DIALOG_GCD_SPECIAL_FOLD_PARK_LOW_CARRIES=1
+```
+
+The trusted nonce-0 run measured average executed Toffoli `1,419,907.236`,
+zero ancilla-garbage batches, 20 classical mismatches, and 15 phase-garbage
+batches. A clean nonce at the rounded Toffoli count would score
+`1,692,529,144`.
+
+## Exact GPU filter
+
+`tools/cuda/island.cu` was ported from the earlier CUDA searcher and corrected
+to match the Rust and Metal models:
+
+- MSB suffix comparisons for apply cleanup;
+- per-step overflow and underflow cleanup widths from the state trailer;
+- separate square and apply phase-risk counts;
+- exact shot windows;
+- zero-phase early rejection for production search.
+
+WMI parity job `57587` passed the serialized SHAKE probe and matched all 65
+Metal survivors over nonces `[0,100)` and shots `[0,256)`, including phase
+breakdown.
+
+## Throughput snapshot
+
+With full 9024-shot zero-phase early rejection and comb-20:
+
+| GPU | Nonces/s |
+|---|---:|
+| RTX 4090 | 3,664 |
+| RTX 3090 | about 3,000 |
+| A100 80 GB | 1,800 |
+| L40S | 4,454 |
+
+Nonce fan-out did not improve throughput. Comb-20 was only about 1.3% faster
+than comb-8, showing that the remaining bottleneck is field and transcript
+arithmetic rather than table lookup.

+## Campaign
+
+WMI array `57599` searches disjoint one-million-nonce shards starting at

+must pass the trusted local evaluator before any submission decision.
