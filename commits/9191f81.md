## Summary

This commit lowers the three 1220-qubit co-binders by two qubits each. All
three changes are exact.

1. **Apply-phase fused fold.** [`572bba4`](572bba4.md) already frees the four
   derived controls (`h`, `xed`, `eord`, `n10`) while the fold's carry runs
   through the high tail. Now the two base controls `e` and `d` are freed as
   well. They are not used in the tail, and they can be rebuilt from the
   overflow bits, which stay live: `d = ovf1 & s2` and `e = ovf1 ⊕ d ⊕ ovf2`.
2. **round84 quotient·c product.** The widest shifted add of the 33-bit
   quotient (shift 4) needed a 29-wire zero pad. It is replaced by a 1-wire
   carry, a dirty-borrowing vented ripple, and a comparator that clears the
   carry wire.
3. **Square.** The windowed square's maximum segment goes from 193 to 191
   bits.

Together these cost +970 Toffoli for −2 qubits.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

1. **`DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_FREED_TAIL_ED=1` (`arith/const_arith.rs`, `rounds/dialog/compressed.rs`).**
   - **Wiring.** `fold_ripple_freed_tail` becomes a wrapper around the new
     `fold_ripple_freed_tail_ed(b, acc, e, d, h, xed, eord, n10, ed, last, is_add)`.
     `dialog_gcd_fused_double_y` and `dialog_gcd_fused_halve_y` now pass
     `ed = Some((ovf1, ovf2, s2))`.
   - **Step 3b.** When the switch is on, after the four derived controls are
     freed and before the tail carries are allocated, the ripple:
     - clears `e` with the 3 CNOTs `cx(ovf1, e); cx(d, e); cx(ovf2, e)` and
       frees it;
     - clears `d` with `hmr(d)` + `cz_if(ovf1, s2)`, at 0 Toffoli, and frees it.
   - **Step 6.** Before the derived controls are rebuilt, it re-acquires `d`
     with `ccx(ovf1, s2, d)` and `e` with the same 3 CNOTs.
   - **Why the formulas hold.**
     - Double: `d = ovf1 & s2` by construction, and `e = ovf1 ⊕ d ⊕ ovf2`
       ([`08c5068`](08c5068.md)).
     - Halve: the code sets `ovf2 = e & s2` and `ovf1 = s2 ? d : e`. When
       `s2 = 1` that gives `ovf1 & s2 = d` and `ovf1 ⊕ d ⊕ ovf2 = e`. When
       `s2 = 0`, `d = 0` (as in the forward double) and `ovf1 = e`. So the same
       two formulas hold.
   - **Effect.** The tail high-water now carries 2 fold ancillae above the
     carries instead of 4. The code comment gives 1220 → 1218 at window 19.
     The cost is 1 CCX per call to rebuild `d`, about +516 over 2 × 258 calls.
2. **`ROUND84_QPROD_VENT_PAD=1` (`arith/multiply.rs`).**
   - `round84_compute_quotient_c_product` and
     `round84_uncompute_quotient_c_product` build `product = q · c` (66 bits)
     from four NAF shifted adds of the 33-bit `q`. They now take the fold's
     `dirty` lanes as an extra argument.
   - Each shifted add used to zero-extend `q` with a `pad` of
     `66 − shift − 33` wires. That is 29 wires at shift 4, and the author
     measured this as the only thing holding the round84 fold at the peak.
   - **New `round84_qprod_shifted_addsub_vented`.** It is used when the pad
     would be at least `ROUND84_QPROD_VENT_PAD_MINW` (default 29) wide, so only
     for shift 4. It works in three steps:
     1. It adds `q` into the 33 low target bits, with the carry out going into
        one new `wrap` wire (`cuccaro_add_low_to_ext_clean`).
     2. It ripples `+wrap` into the 29 high bits with Gidney's 2-clean
        dirty-ancilla adder, `venting::ciadd_dirty_2clean_classical`, borrowing
        the idle `dirty` lanes.
     3. It clears `wrap` with `cmp_lt_into(new_low, q)`, because the carry out
        of an add is exactly "the new low part is below `q`". The subtract
        version mirrors this with `cisub_...` and an X-wrapped compare.
   - **Effect.** The transient drops from about `product + 30` wires to about
     `product + 3`.
   - **Cost.** A coherent 33-bit add, a vented ripple and a compare, instead of
     one measured 62-bit add: roughly +150 to +200 Toffoli per build. With
     `ROUND84_KEEP_QUOTIENT_PRODUCT=1` ([`997dbd6`](997dbd6.md)) the product is
     built once in the fold and unbuilt once in the unfold, so there are two
     such builds.
   - The comment above the helper says venting is needed in "all four product
     builds", which was written for the route without the kept product.
3. **`SQUARE_ROW_MAX_SEG`: 193 → 191.** Rows 64 and 65 are now also added in
   two windows. That narrows the windowed square's largest transient source
   register by 2, at the cost of their boundary-carry cleanup (18-bit compares).
4. **`DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE`** (the top-of-route pin, which is the one that takes
   effect): 307003737209 → 402004742830.

**Toffoli bookkeeping.** About +516 from rebuilding `d`, about +300 to +400
from the two vented shift-4 builds, and about +100 from the two extra windowed
rows. That is roughly the measured +970. The split is an estimate.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **Freeing `e` and `d` is exact.**
  - Neither is a control above bit 33, so the tail carries do not depend on
    them.
  - `ovf1`, `ovf2` and `s2` are unchanged across the ripple, so the rebuilt
    values equal the originals.
  - The measured clear of `d` is phase-exact because `d = ovf1 & s2` at the
    moment it is measured.
- **The vented quotient-product add is exact.**
  - It computes the same `product[4..] ± q` as the padded add.
  - The dirty-borrowing adder restores the borrowed lanes.
  - The comparator returns `wrap` to 0 exactly.
- **Windowing two more rows is exact.**
- **Probabilistic parts.** None are added. The inherited ones (the square's
  18-bit cleanup compare, the GCD envelope and the carry windows) are
  unchanged, but the op stream changed, so the test set is new.
- The shipped nonce `402004742830` draws a clean set: 9,024 of 9,024 shots,
  with 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. A broken identity in the
  new code would show as phase or ancilla garbage. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

All three changes shorten the lifetime of ancillae or borrow wires instead of
allocating them:
- `e` and `d` are freed and recomputed;
- the 29-wire pad is replaced by a dirty-borrowing vented ripple;
- the square uses a narrower window.

That is ancilla-lifetime / venting, which matches the published label.
