## Summary

In this tree the ping-pong divider interleaves walk and replay: rounds
`r1..=r2` of the walk run one at a time, each followed (divide) or preceded
(multiply) by its replay cell. The replay cells already size their chunked
carry ladders to stay under a peak budget `SUB4_PP_PEAK`. The walk add of
those rounds did not: it always used one full-width carry ladder. Near `r1`
the walk registers are still about 87 bits wide, so tape + walk registers +
walk ladder + the two 256-bit coefficient registers reached the peak.

This commit gives those walk adds a ladder budget too. When the modelled
footprint of a walk round is over the plan peak, the add is split into a tiny
leading chunk and one wide chunk, which takes one or two carry wires off the
ladder. The repair at that boundary is exact. With the walk no longer
binding, the plan peak drops to 1277 and the product-register square's ladder
budget drops from 248 to 247, so every phase fits in one fewer qubit. The
tighter budgets cost 332 average Toffoli; the qubit is worth about 740.

## What changed and why it saves a qubit

All in `src/point_add/pingpong_div.rs` unless noted.

1. **New `set_walk_ladder_for_peak(plan, round)`.**
   - It models the live count of an interleaved walk round as
     `round + 3·width + 2·N`: the tape so far, the two walk registers of
     `width = value_width(round)` bits, a full `width − 1` carry ladder, and
     the coefficient and numerator registers.
   - If that exceeds `plan.peak`, it calls
     `set_ladder(width − 1 − excess)`. Otherwise it clears the ladder target,
     so non-binding rounds keep the cheaper `signed_add_wrapping_sigma`.
   - It is called before each interleaved `walk_round` in the Divide branch
     and before each interleaved `walk_back_round` in the Multiply branch,
     with `clear_chunks()` right after. `SUB4_PP_DISABLE_WALK_CHUNK_TO_PEAK`
     turns it off.
   - With the shipped widths, the rounds that trigger at peak 1277 are 505,
     506, 508 and 511 (width 85–87, excess 1–2). Each fires once per
     traversal.
2. **`signed_add_wrapping`: route through the chunked adder when a ladder
   target is set.** If `ladder_target_now()` is `Some`, the add becomes the
   sign-conjugated `cx(sign, target…); add_chunked_measured(source, target, None); cx(sign, target…)`.
   - `add_chunked_measured` then uses `chunk_layout(width, target)`. For an
     excess of 1–2 that layout is a leading chunk of 1 + excess bits plus one
     wide chunk.
   - The boundary carry is erased with `hmr` and a conditioned compare of the
     producing chunk's top `min(REPLAY_CHUNK_COMPARE, w)` bits. The leading
     chunk is only 2–3 bits and has no carry-in, so that compare covers the
     whole chunk and is **exact**. See
     [adders-and-carries](../concepts/adders-and-carries.md) and
     [comparator-and-width-truncation](../concepts/comparator-and-width-truncation.md).
   - The cost is a few Toffoli per affected round: the chunk adder lacks the
     sigma adder's two free leading carries, and the tiny repair runs on half
     the shots.
3. **`plan()` retune.** `SUB4_PP_R1` 509 → 504, `SUB4_PP_R2` 610 → 608,
   `SUB4_PP_PEAK` 1278 → 1277.
   - `SUB4_PP_PEAK` is the budget that `allowance(plan, tape_len, walk_width)`
     hands to every replay cell's carry ladder, so all replay ladders get one
     wire less.
   - Moving `r1`/`r2` earlier changes which rounds are batch-replayed and which
     are interleaved.
4. **`SQUARE_LADDER`: 248 → 247** in
   `src/point_add/trailmix_ludicrous/square/product_register.rs`. This is the
   live carry-ladder budget of the square's wide (≥ 200-bit) adds
   (`add_chunked_measured_budgeted`). The square is a co-binder of the peak,
   so it must come down too. See
   [peak-qubits-and-budgets](../concepts/peak-qubits-and-budgets.md).
5. **Tail nonce** (`SUB4_PINGPONG_TAIL_NONCE` default in `build()`,
   `src/point_add/mod.rs`): 12145000000 → 8004485.

Why Toffoli goes up: smaller ladder budgets force more or wider chunk
boundaries in the replay and in the square, and each boundary has a measured
repair. The four chunked walk adds per traversal are a small part of the
+332.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **The chunked walk add is exact.** The add itself is exact, and its only
  boundary repair compares the whole leading chunk.
- **Budget changes are layout changes.** The replay and square adds stay
  exact in value. Some of their boundary repairs compare only the top
  `REPLAY_CHUNK_COMPARE` = 23 bits of a wide chunk; those are approximate
  (wrong on a top-bit tie, about 2^−23 per call, a **phase** failure). A
  tighter budget can add such repairs, which raises λ a little.
- The r1/r2 move does not change any arithmetic, only the order of
  operations and the ladder sizes.
- The shipped nonce `8004485` draws a clean set: 0 classical, 0 phase and
  0 ancilla failures. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The qubit comes from bounding a live carry ladder at the peak and lowering
the budgets that every peak owner plans against. That is ancilla-lifetime /
venting, the change-type used for budget retunes. The runner-up would be an
adder change, but the adder is not new: the walk just reuses the replay's
chunked adder.
