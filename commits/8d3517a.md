## Summary

Each replay cell of the ping-pong divider folds a small correction (one of
`{−f, 0, +f, +2f}`) into the low 54 bits of its target with a ripple-carry
chain, `fused_fold_maskfree`. All 52 carries of that chain are live at once.
When the replay budget is pushed down to 1274, in the tightest rounds this
fold, not the chunked add, becomes the widest thing alive.

This commit adds an exact two-segment version of the fold. It runs the low
23 positions, erases all their carries except the boundary carry, then runs
the high positions with the boundary as carry-in. Afterwards it erases the
boundary with an exact, not truncated, comparison. The two ladders are never
live together, so the fold needs fewer wires. It is used only in rounds
whose ladder budget is below 60. With that, the replay budget and the square
ladder can both drop by one: 1275 → 1274 qubits. The commit also narrows the
endpoint fold window from 28 to 20 to win back Toffoli. The net is +716
Toffoli, just under the break-even of about 739.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

All in `src/point_add/pingpong_div.rs` unless noted.

### 1. New `fused_fold_maskfree_segmented` (+176 lines)

`split = min(replay_chunk_compare(), width − 3)`, at least 2. With the
defaults (`REPLAY_CHUNK_COMPARE = 23`, `REPLAY_FOLD_WINDOW = 54`) the split
is at 23.

1. **Low segment, positions 1..22.** The same ripple as the monolithic
   fold: one CCX per carry, using one roving `operand` wire for the selector
   XOR. Then it runs the backward pass for positions 1..21 only. That writes
   the sum bits and erases those carries with `hmr` plus `cz_if`, then frees
   them. Position 22's carry, the `boundary`, is kept.
2. **High segment, positions 23..52.** A fresh ladder of `width − 1 − split`
   = 30 carries, with `boundary` as its carry-in; the last carry feeds the
   top bit 53. Forward pass, top sum bit,
   backward erase, free.
3. **Boundary erase.** `hmr(boundary, phase)`, then it materialises the
   selected low operand in a temporary `split`-wide register, `selected`, by
   CX from the selector bits. Then
   `cmp_lt_phase_conditioned(acc[..split], selected, phase)`, and the
   temporary is un-materialised and freed.
   - This is exact. For a sum `s = a + o mod 2^k`, the carry out is exactly
     `[s < o]`. The compare covers all `split` low bits, so there is no top-k
     truncation.
   - `acc[..split]` already holds the final low sum, because the high
     segment only writes higher positions.

**Live wires.** The monolithic fold holds the operand plus 52 carries. The
segmented one holds, at most, the operand plus 22 low carries; then the
boundary, operand and 30 high carries; then, for the repair, the 23-wire
`selected` register plus the compare's 23 carries and carry-in. That stays
under the 60-wire trigger. See [modular-fold](../concepts/modular-fold.md)
and [venting-and-wire-loans](../concepts/venting-and-wire-loans.md)
("Rescheduling").

**Cost.** The boundary repair is a `split`-bit compare that runs only when
`phase = 1`, about 11.5 executed Toffoli per call. The monolithic fold
erased that carry for free.

**Dispatch.** `fused_fold_maskfree` calls the segmented form only if
`ladder_target_now()`, the per-round ladder budget the replay plan has set,
is below 60, and `width >= 6`. Its comment says "Q1275 and wider routes
remain byte-identical". `SUB4_PP_SEGMENTED_FOLD_FORCE` forces it on and
`SUB4_PP_NO_SEGMENTED_FOLD` turns it off.

### 2. Lower budgets

| knob | base | new |
|---|---|---|
| `SUB4_PP_PEAK` (`plan()`) | 1275 | 1274 |
| `SUB4_PP_R1` | 289 | 286 |
| `SUB4_PP_R2` | 598 | 603 |
| `SQUARE_LADDER` (`trailmix_ludicrous/square/product_register.rs`) | 245 | 244 |

As in [`0b82781`](0b82781.md) and [`84a93cd`](84a93cd.md), the replay
chunk ladders are sized to `peak − (tape + 2·256 + 2·walk width)`, and the
square's wide adds to `SQUARE_LADDER`. Both peak owners drop by one wire.
That costs extra chunk boundaries, and therefore extra truncated repairs. See
[peak-qubits-and-budgets](../concepts/peak-qubits-and-budgets.md).

### 3. Endpoint fold window: 28 → 20 (`endpoint_fold_window()` default)

- This shortens the constant-add carry chains in `conditional_mod_negate`,
  `mod_halve_pm` and `mod_double_pm` by 8 positions each, one CCX each per
  call, executed on every shot.
- The escape probability per call is about 2^−20. Over about 8 calls per
  shot and 9,024 shots that is about 0.07 expected failures per draw.
- `d1bb787` made the same narrowing on another branch.

### 4. Tail nonce (`src/point_add/mod.rs`)

`SUB4_PINGPONG_TAIL_NONCE` default 23157411 → 25125857.

**Net.** The segmented boundary repairs and the extra chunk repairs cost
Toffoli; the endpoint narrowing gives some back. The total is +716 T for
−1 qubit. The break-even is 941,683 / 1274 ≈ 739 T, so the score drops by
only 28,783.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **The segmented fold is exact.** It computes the same sum as the
  monolithic fold, including the same window truncation at the top. Its
  boundary erase uses an exact full-width compare, so it adds no hard inputs.
- **The lower budgets** add a few truncated chunk-boundary repairs, so λ goes
  up slightly. A failure shows as phase garbage.
- **The endpoint window at 20** adds about 0.07 λ. A failure there is a
  wrong value, so a classical mismatch.
- The shipped nonce `25125857` draws a clean test set: 0 classical, 0 phase
  and 0 ancilla failures. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The qubit comes from reordering when the fold's carry ancillas are allocated
and freed, an exact lifetime change, together with lower budgets. That is
ancilla-lifetime / venting. The endpoint window cut is a secondary fold
truncation for Toffoli.
