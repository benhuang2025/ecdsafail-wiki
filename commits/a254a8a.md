## Summary

The trailmix circuit's chunked controlled adders erase each chunk's carry-out
by measuring it and then, on about half the shots, recomputing it with a
comparison over the whole chunk. This commit compares only the top 22 bits of
each chunk, except in the first 24 erase calls. That is the main saving. The
note measures emitted CCX going from 1,394,540 to 1,350,475.

The author also measured that the base circuit was far from clean on a random
nonce: λ = 23.3 expected failing shots per draw, so a fresh clean nonce had
odds of about 1 in 10^10. Most of that came from GCD walks that had not
converged after 258 divsteps. The commit therefore raises `ITERS` to 261. On
its own that costs about +2.3% score, but it makes a clean nonce findable
(λ ≈ 9). The longer walk renumbers every call counter, so all the census-keyed
"structurally dead" gate tables are switched off automatically. A new strip,
mined over 1e9 inputs on the new stream, wins most of that back: 9,268 deleted
gates plus 2,050 CCX→CX / CCZ→CZ downgrades. Net: −6,934 Toffoli for +1 qubit.

## What changed and why it saves Toffoli

1. **Carry-erase compare cap (the main lever), `trailmix_ludicrous/gidney.rs`.**
   - `controlled_erase_carry_gated_impl` erases a chunk's carry-out. It does
     `hmr(carry, bit)`, then under `push_condition(bit)` runs `compare_geq` over
     the chunk's sum and addend, with a phase deposit, to cancel the phase the
     measurement left. See [measurement-uncompute](../concepts/measurement-uncompute.md)
     and [adders-and-carries](../concepts/adders-and-carries.md).
   - It is used by the chunked controlled adds with carry-out
     (`controlled_hybrid_add_cout_refs`, called from `arith::controlled_mod_add_k`
     and `gcd::controlled_add_active_cout`). The note says these carry the apply
     passes' 256-bit register adds, and that their cost is exactly
     `2n + 1 + l + m`, where `l` is the erase compares.
   - New `cout_erase_cap()` reads `TLM_COUT_ERASE_CAP`, and
     `cout_erase_cap_call_in_window()` reads `TLM_COUT_ERASE_CAP_CALLS = "lo:hi"`.
     When a chunk has `s > w` bits and the call index is in the window, the
     compare runs only on `a[s−w..]`, `b[s−w..]`, with a fresh |0⟩ carry-in
     (`zcin`) instead of the real one. It costs `w` CCX instead of `s`, and it
     executes on about half the shots.
   - The census drop tables inside the compare are switched off in the capped
     branch: new `comparator::compare_geq_cin_middle_keyed(.., drops: None)`, and
     the CCZ deposit is always emitted. Those tables were certified for the
     untruncated chain.
   - `build()` sets `TLM_COUT_ERASE_CAP=22` and `TLM_COUT_ERASE_CAP_CALLS=24:9999`.
     The note says capping every call gives 141/141 phase-garbage batches. In the
     first ~24 calls the Bezout values are still small, so the top 22 bits of
     both operands are zero and carry no information. Exempting them costs about
     1% of the saving.
2. **`ITERS` 258 → 261 (`trailmix_ludicrous/schedule.rs`).**
   - The note's measured convergence tail, in expected mismatches per 9,024
     shots: 258 → 5.228, 259 → 2.453, 260 → 1.114, 261 → 0.483. `SCHED_J2` and
     `GAP_J2` get three more entries, at width 11 and window 12.
   - New `BAKED_ITERS = 258` and `baked_artifacts_valid()` (`ITERS == BAKED_ITERS`).
   - The eight baked per-call schedule vectors are read by a sequential cursor,
     one block per walk pass. New `widen_sched_blocks()` in
     `trailmix_ludicrous/mod.rs` pads each pass's block with its terminal entry,
     so every (pass, divstep) still reads its own value. Reverse blocks are
     padded at the front. The comment says this keeps the peak at 1153 instead
     of 1155. At `ITERS = BAKED_ITERS` it is the identity.
   - New `drops_off_family(fam)` (`trailmix_ludicrous/mod.rs`) returns true when
     `!baked_artifacts_valid()`, or when `TLM_DROPS_OFF` or `TLM_DROPS_OFF_ONLY`
     says so. It is checked at the top of every census-keyed
     `*_has_structurally_dead_*` predicate in `arith.rs`, `comparator.rs`,
     `fused.rs`, `gcd.rs` and `gidney.rs` (19 families, for example `FFG`,
     `CUCCARO`, `CMPCIN`, `GCDFSW`, `GCDSHIFT`, `THRSUM`). At ITERS 261 all of
     them are off. That includes the W1155 cswap and E251 shift tables of
     [`5b834ba`](5b834ba.md). The reason: those tables are keyed by per-helper
     call counters, and three more divsteps renumber them, so a key would name a
     live gate.
3. **Proven top-edge Fredkin skip, `gcd.rs`.**
   - `skip_top_zero_controlled_shift_edge` is now on by default
     (`TLM_GCD_SKIP_TOP_ZERO_SHIFT_EDGE=0` turns it off; before, it was off unless
     the variable was set).
   - It drops the last Fredkin of every controlled one-bit shift ladder. When
     the control is 1, both bits it would exchange are 0: `v[0]` because only an
     even `v` is halved, and `v[w−1]` because the step's unconditional halving
     already rotated a zero there. The exception is the step-0 `t1` shift, the
     first halving, so it is kept.
   - The comment counts 1,176 shift Fredkins suppressed by the census tables in
     the base. 964 of them are these provable edges. With the tables retired,
     this rule keeps those, at no added risk. The note's "68 Fredkins removed
     under a proof" is the rest of these edges, the ones the tables had missed.
4. **Re-mined strip with downgrades (`deep_strip_keys.rs`, `apply_deep_strip_identity` in `mod.rs`).**
   - The table is regenerated from a census of 1,000,120,320 random input pairs,
     run on the new ITERS-261 stream with the strip and all 19 families off.
   - `DEAD_KEYS` has 9,268 entries, which are deleted. New `DOWNGRADE_KEYS` has
     2,050 entries `(…, act)`. For these the predicate `cond & q1 & !q2` (or the
     mirror) was never seen, meaning one control implies the other. Then
     CCX(c2, c1, t) = CX(survivor, t), and CCZ becomes CZ, which the scorer does
     not charge. `act` 1 or 2 says which control survives.
   - Every key now carries a 7th field, `tuple_occupancy`: how often its operand
     tuple occurred in the census stream. A first pass counts the tuples in the
     current stream, and any key whose count differs is discarded with a
     warning instead of being applied. The comment says a slid ordinal once
     caused 7,535/9,024 classical mismatches.
   - The `SUB4_APPLY_D2_STRIP` branch is removed. See
     [dead-gate-strip](../concepts/dead-gate-strip.md).
5. **Smaller and diagnostic edits.**
   - `arith::msbs()` reads `TLM_MSBS`, defaulting to `PAD`, and replaces `MSBS` in
     the final reduction compares. It is unset, so the value does not change.
   - `install_q1153_submission_defaults` no longer overrides `TLM_APPLY_ADD_SKIP_LASTK`,
     `TLM_FFG_MAX_G` or `TLM_FOLD_CHUNK_ZERO_CIN` when they are already set.
     `mod.rs` already sets them to the same values, so nothing changes.
   - Diagnostics that do not change the stream: `TLM_GCD_SHIFT_PROVEN_ONLY`,
     `TRACE_TLM_TOF`, per-divstep trace-context stamps, and the new
     `dirtyscan.rs` reset/phase audit (`TLM_DIRTY_SCAN`, `TLM_DIRTY_SCAN_FINAL`),
     all off by default.
   - Four `*.rs.orig` backup copies under `trailmix_ludicrous/`, which are not
     compiled.
   - Tail nonce: `SUB4_TAIL_NONCE` default 4001397000397 → 5150000095.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **Erase cap: probabilistic.** The capped compare is wrong when the top 22
  bits of sum and addend tie and the low part borrows, about 2^-22 per call. A
  wrong compare leaves the measurement's phase uncancelled, which shows up in
  the phase channel.
- **ITERS 261 lowers λ.** It makes the remaining non-convergence rarer.
  Retiring the drop tables is exact, since it only puts gates back.
- **Top-edge skip: exact**, by the argument above.
- **Strip: empirical.** "Never seen in 1e9 inputs" is not a proof, and this
  applies to the downgrades as well as the deletions. The occupancy tripwire
  catches slid ordinals. It does not catch a gate that fires on an input the
  census never drew.
- **λ.** The note measures the base over 700 random nonces: mean 18.1 classical
  mismatches and 12.6 phase-garbage batches, with zero clean runs. Its fit gives
  λ_total = 23.3, P(clean) ≈ 7.7e-11. This commit sits at λ ≈ 9, about 3,000
  candidate nonces per winner. The author also built a stack at ITERS 258 that
  scores 1,497,088,655 but has λ ≈ 17 (about 1e7 nonces), and did not ship it.
- The shipped nonce `5150000095` draws a clean set: 0 classical, 0 phase and 0
  ancilla failures. See [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The largest Toffoli cut is the narrower carry-erase compare, which is
comparator/width truncation. ITERS 261 goes the other way (it buys λ), and the
re-mined strip mostly pays that back. Dead-gate/self-inverse removal is the
runner-up.
