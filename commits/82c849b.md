## Summary

The author measured mochimodev's Skywalk at a Lambda of 18.30, above the
limit of 18 they chose to keep. Lambda is the expected number of failing shots
in one 9,024-shot test draw. So the package first buys error back: it widens
the walk envelopes by one bit on 34 divide ticks and 33 multiply ticks, which
costs about 81 Toffoli. It then spends that room only on exact savings, plus
two small sells.

The exact savings are:
- fusions that merge the FD payload's doubling and negations with the
  surrounding `coord_y_sub` and `coord_y_sub_final` ops;
- classical-operand adders and compares that drop one Toffoli each;
- more reuse of an AND product the walk has already computed (codec shares);
- a post-pass with 57 offline SAT-proven CCX → CX rewrites.

Qubits stay at 1174. The net is −326 Toffoli. The note puts the final Lambda
at 17.88 ± 0.03, measured on 16,384 nonces.

## What changed and why it saves Toffoli

The note builds the package in five stages, sky1 to sky5. The Toffoli figures
below are the note's means over many nonces, not the score's single nonce.
Every knob is a new or changed entry in `install_skywalk_submission_recipe()`
in `src/point_add/mod.rs`.

### sky1: Lambda buy-back, two sells, 57 rewrites (−23.5 T)

1. **Wider envelopes** (+80.6 T, copied data).
   - `heo::config()` and `config_mul()` in `heo.rs` now `include_str!` the new
     files `skywalk_data/sky5_env_div_gN.d.35.txt` (393 ticks) and
     `sky5_env_mul_gN.m.35.txt` (392 ticks), in place of `extended-middle-0/1`.
   - The `HEO_ENVELOPE` variables still name the old paths, but the loader only
     tests that they are set.
   - Against the old envelopes, no tick is narrower, and each changed tick gains
     exactly one bit in `esw`, `ead` or both:
     - divide: 34 ticks, +24 `esw` bits and +17 `ead` bits in total, mostly
       ticks 15–19 and 210–257;
     - multiply: 33 ticks, +25 / +13, mostly ticks 15–19 and 210–237.
   - A wider rail costs Toffoli on that tick but overflows less often, so
     classical failures fall. The note says the buy took Lambda from 18.30 to
     about 17.75, about 145 Toffoli per unit of Lambda.
2. **`HEO_FIT_K`: 24 → 23.** This is the window of the room-fitted exact adds
   (`heo_split_ripple` in `modular.rs`, `HEO_FIT_MODE=win`). Each chunk boundary
   carry is erased by `erase_with_compare` over the top `K` bits of the chunk,
   or exactly when the chunk is a leading chunk of at most `K` bits. One bit
   narrower means fewer Toffoli and about twice the error per boundary.
3. **`HEO_PIN_SQ_ASM_TAIL`: 20 → 19.** In the square's `add_cross`
   (`square.rs`), the carry ripples only `E` bits past the cross word. The
   note says it misses a carry with probability about 2^-E. Together, sells 2
   and 3 save about 47 T for +0.057 Lambda.
4. **SAT-proven rewrites** (−57 T, copied data). There is a new block in
   `build()`, after `mabsorb` and before the tail nonce. It reads
   `skywalk_data/sky5_ycd_rewrite.txt` with `include_str!`. Each line is
   `widx c1 c2 t cst w1,w2,…` (or `-` for no wires), and it states that the
   CCX at op index `widx`, with controls `{c1, c2}` and target `t`, can be
   replaced by `CX(w1→t), CX(w2→t), …` plus `X(t)` if `cst = 1`. That holds
   because an offline SAT check proved `c1·c2 = w1 ⊕ w2 ⊕ … ⊕ cst` on every
   reachable state at that point. The loader then works like this:
   - It sorts the rows by index, descending, so each splice leaves the lower
     indices valid.
   - It searches shifts `d` in [−2,000,000, 2,000,000] for one under which
     every row's `widx + d` is an unconditioned CCX on exactly those wires. It
     asserts that exactly one such shift exists. A rigid shift of the whole
     stream is tolerated, but any other upstream change panics the build.
   - It replaces each CCX. Every row removes one unconditioned CCX, so the
     saving is exactly 1 Toffoli per row on every shot.
   - The 57 rows sit at ops 4,621,958 to 8,924,677. They need between 1 and 44
     CX each, and 23 of them also add the `X`.

### sky2: divide batch layout and multiply reverse codec share (−61.5 T)

- **`HEO_R2D`: 375 → 382.** The divide leg runs ticks below `r2d` as fused
  ticks, with the cell and route between rail ticks. Ticks from `r2d` to 392
  run as a trailing batch after the walk. The batch drops from 18 cells to 11,
  so less of the group codec is unpacked and repacked.
- **`HEO_DIV_LIFO_N`: 1 → 2.** The divide batch leaves its last `n + 1` groups
  raw, because the walk-back unpacks them first anyway.
- The note measured these two as error-neutral, −22.5 T.
- **`GO_MULREV2_SHARE=1`, `GO_MULREV_SHARE=1`** (new, in `reverse_tick`,
  `heo_carry.rs`). In 8ee337c the reverse codec share ran only on the divide
  walk-back (`label == "divrev"`). Now it also runs on the multiply's two
  rails-only reverse passes. When the unpack rebuilds a group, it keeps the
  product it already computed (`unpack_compatible_retained`) and hands it to
  the reverse rail add as its first carry (`reverse_carries`). That saves one
  CCX per site. The note counts 39 new sites, −39 T, exact by the invariant
  `typ_prev · sign_prev = 0`. `fama_rev`'s assert is relaxed so the shared
  carry is also accepted in `SplitMode::Exact`.

### sky3 and the final selection: divide forward codec share (−36 T, then −4 T)

- **`GO_DIVFWD_SHARE=1` with `GO_DIVFWD_SHARE_T`**, a list of 44 divide ticks
  (2, 12, 17, …, 387, all ≡ 2 mod 5). In `forward_tick`, the forward share,
  which in 8ee337c ran only on the multiply forward pass, now also runs on the
  listed divide ticks. The rail add's retained first carry gives the product
  `h2·typ3`, which replaces the first CCX of the next `pack3`
  (`pack_compatible_shared`). That saves one CCX per site.
- **`GO_SHARE_ROOMFIX=1`**: while a shared product is pending, `fama_add`
  counts one less wire of room, because the kept wire occupies a slot.
- The final list is the note's re-tuned "dfsel_s4" selection (−4 T more).

### sky4: FD payload fusions (−144 T)

These live in new functions in `heo_carry.rs`.

- **`R4_FDP_FUSE=1`** (`fd_payload_div_r4`, `fd_payload_div_inv_r4`). The
  divide's FD payload used to do three things after copying `Sig` into `Del`:
  `mod_double(Del)`, the route on `A = NOT(X_sign ⊕ Y_sign)`, and two
  conditional negations. Now:
  - Both negations move in front of the route.
  - Sig's control becomes `cS = ys ⊕ (A ∧ NOT o_0)`. That costs one AND,
    uncomputed by measurement.
  - Del's doubling and negation become one ladder
    (`r4_fdp_double_neg`): `Del ← ctl ? p − 2·Del : 2·Del`, as a rotation plus a
    single selected constant ladder over the `go_fs("GO_FG_P")` window, with
    `ctl = NOT o_0`.
  - The multiply's payload inverse is the exact mirror.
- **`R4_YSUB_FUSE=1`** (`r4_ysub_head`, `r4_ysub_tail`). `coord_y_sub` now
  only computes `y ← ~y + oy` and keeps the overflow `ov` live. At divide tick
  0, once `X_sign`, `Y_sign` and `o_0` exist, one window ladder adds
  `K = ov·f − cS·(f − 1) (mod 2^fs)`. Then `ov` is erased with the usual
  measured compare, and the closing complement is XOR-ed with `NOT cS`. This
  fuses `coord_y_sub`'s fold correction with Sig's conditional negation, which
  the note prices at 49 T on its own, so `r4_fdp_neg_sig` is skipped.
  `build_point_add()` calls `r4_ysub_head` in phase `coord_y_sub`.
- **`R3_S1_TICKS=5,7,8,268`**, which replaces the old hard-coded list
  `[5, 7, 8, 260, 272]`. It picks the divide forward ticks whose S1 ANDs are
  deferred past the mid-tick cell (`HEO_CELL_HELPER_S1`). Ticks 260 and 272
  leave the list and tick 268 joins it.

### sky5: y-final fusion and classical operands (−59.7 T)

- **`R4_YFIN_FUSE=1`, `R5_YFIN2=1`** (`r4_yfin_stash`, `r4_yfin_tail`,
  `r5_yfin2_body`). `build_point_add()` stashes `oy` before `multiply`. At the
  end of the multiply's FD payload inverse, Del is zero, so it is freed, and
  `Sig ← neg_cS(Sig) − oy` is computed in one pass:
  - XOR frame on `NOT cS`;
  - add `oy` as a classical operand;
  - one window ladder `K = ov·f + cS·(f − 1)`;
  - erase `ov`.
  The phase `coord_y_sub_final` then only asserts that `oy` was consumed. This
  removes one more separate negation ladder.
- **`R5_CBITS=15`** (bitmask: 1 `coord_x_sub` via
  `fd_coordinate::retained_coord_sub`, 2 the y-sub head, 4 and 8 the two
  back-seam legs in `back_seam.rs`).
  - These adds of a classical value run `modular::r5_ripple_add_cbits`, a
    Gidney ladder with the classical bits applied to the carry wires through
    classically conditioned X gates.
  - It needs no 256-qubit temp register.
  - Bit 0's carry `a_0 · acc_0` is a classically conditioned CX, so each add
    uses one CCX fewer.
- **`R5_CCMP=23`** (bitmask: 1 `j_fuse::erase_x_carry`, 2 the y-sub erase, 4
  the back-seam erase, 16 the y-final erase). These overflow erasures use the
  new `compare::erase_with_compare_v0`:
  - `hmr` on the overflow, then the phase-conditioned compare
    `cmp_lt_phase_v0`.
  - Because the compared operand's bit 0 is a known classical bit `b0`, the
    first carry `u0 · NOT b0` is a CX plus a CX conditioned on `b0`, instead
    of a CCX.
  - The compare runs only when the measurement returns 1, about half the shots,
    so each site saves about 0.5 Toffoli on average.
- **`R5_CBITS_PAD=1`, `R5_CBITS_PAD_ALL=1`**: allocate and free a pad after the
  back-seam add, so the free list looks as it did when the temp register
  existed. This changes wire numbering only, not gates.

### Other edits

- `mod.rs`: new `clear_process_env()`, called first in `build()`. It removes
  every inherited environment variable before the recipe is installed, so no
  `HEO_PIN_*`, `GO_*`, `R*_*`, `SKY_*` or other host variable can change the

- `builder.rs`: `r3_peak()`, peak bookkeeping for the new trace switches

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **Exact by construction:**
  - the fusions `R4_FDP_FUSE`, `R4_YSUB_FUSE`, `R4_YFIN_FUSE` / `R5_YFIN2`;
  - the classical-operand adders `R5_CBITS`;
  - the codec shares, which rest on an invariant the note checked on every
    site;
  - the 57 rewrite rows, which are SAT-proven on reachable states and asserted
    against the op stream at build time.
- **`R5_CCMP`** keeps the same predicate and the same measurements, so it is
  exact too.
- **`YFIN2`** merges a fold and an erase window. The note saw classical
  mismatch counts differ by ±1 shot on 12 of 4,096 nonces, with a mean
  difference of +0.001.
- **The two sells are probabilistic.** `HEO_FIT_K` 23 doubles the miss rate of
  each windowed boundary repair, which shows up as a phase failure.
  `SQ_ASM_TAIL` 19 lets a rare carry escape the square, which shows up as a
  classical failure.
- **The envelope buy lowers error.** Wider rails overflow less often.
- **The rewrite table is tied to this exact op stream.** Any later change that
  shifts the stream non-rigidly makes the build panic at the unique-shift
  assert. 800ba12 deletes the block for this reason.
- **Lambda.** The note gives 17.884 ± 0.031 on 16,384 nonces, below its own
  limit of 18 and mochimodev's 19.5. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).
- The shipped nonce `5000500937876` draws a clean set: 9,024 of 9,024 shots,
  with 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. The average is 945,923.877
  Toffoli.

## Classification rationale

The package mixes several levers, and the note gives a split. The fusions
(sky4, −144 T, plus the YFIN2 part of sky5) are the largest piece, so the
commit is filed as Fusion → gate/op fusion. The runners-up are the codec
shares (−75 T, which also reuse an already computed product) and the SAT rows
(−57 T, host-derived specialization, the type used for
[`0642f52`](0642f52.md)). The envelope buy and the two sells cancel to about
+34 T.
