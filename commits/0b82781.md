## Summary

In this tree the qubit peak is a budget. The ping-pong replay sizes the
carry ladder of every chunked add to the room left under
`SUB4_PP_PEAK = 1278`, and the product-register square sizes its wide adds
to a ladder budget `SQUARE_LADDER = 248`, which also lands at 1278. A lower
budget forces more, narrower chunks. Each extra chunk boundary costs a
measured erase with a truncated phase-repair compare.

This commit lowers both owners together: the replay budget to 1276, and the
square ladder to 246. It also moves the interleave checkpoints
(`r1` 298 → 292, `r2` 613 → 608) to fit the new budget. The peak drops by
2 qubits for +1,469 Toffoli. To win back some Toffoli it also adds the exact
dead-low carry elision of `33e2b34` to the five endpoint constant adds.
Since one qubit is worth about 736 Toffoli here (939,026 / 1276), the score
improves, but only by 3,608.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

### 1. Replay budget and checkpoints (`plan()`, `src/point_add/pingpong_div.rs`)

| knob | base | new |
|---|---|---|
| `SUB4_PP_R1` | 298 | 292 |
| `SUB4_PP_R2` | 613 | 608 |
| `SUB4_PP_PEAK` | 1278 | 1276 |

How the budget is used:

- **Plan structure.** In the divide traversal, rounds below `r1` are walked
  first and then replayed in one batch. Rounds `r1..=r2` are replayed beside
  their own walk round. Rounds above `r2` are replayed after the walk, at
  the terminal state. The multiply traversal mirrors this in doubling order.
  See [pingpong-division](../concepts/pingpong-division.md) ("The peak and
  why ladders matter").
- **Chunk count.** At each replay round, `pick_chunks` computes
  `allowance(plan, tape_len, walk_width) = peak − (tape_len + 2·N + 2·walk_width)`.
  By default `ladder_for_allowance` turns that, minus the cell's own extra
  wires, into a live-ladder budget, and `chunk_layout` picks chunk
  boundaries that fit it. (Only under `SUB4_PP_LEGACY_LADDER` does
  `chunks_for_allowance` pick a chunk count `k` in 3..8.)
- **Effect of a lower peak.** Two wires less means some rounds need one more
  chunk. Each extra chunk adds a boundary carry that is erased with `hmr`
  plus a `REPLAY_CHUNK_COMPARE`-bit `cmp_lt_phase_conditioned`, which costs
  Toffoli on about half the shots.
- **Checkpoints.** Moving `r1` and `r2` down changes where the batch and
  trailing replays happen, and so how long the tape and how wide the walk
  registers are when each replay cell runs. The new values were chosen to
  fit under 1276.

### 2. Square ladder budget (`trailmix_ludicrous/square/product_register.rs`)

`const SQUARE_LADDER: usize = 248` → `246`.
- `add_full` runs every add of at least `SQUARE_CHUNK_MIN = 200` bits
  through `add_chunked_measured_budgeted(…, budget)`, so its live carry
  ladder holds at most `SQUARE_LADDER` wires.
- The square is the other owner of the peak. Without this change it would
  still reach 1278. Two fewer ladder wires again means more chunk boundaries
  in the square's wide adds.
- See [squaring-and-karatsuba](../concepts/squaring-and-karatsuba.md) and
  [peak-qubits-and-budgets](../concepts/peak-qubits-and-budgets.md)
  ("Co-binders").

### 3. Exact dead-low carry elision (`arith/const_arith.rs`, +182 lines)

- New `dead_low_carry_run` and the
  `c{add,sub}_nbit_const_direct_trunc_fast_dead_low` helpers, the same as in
  [`33e2b34`](33e2b34.md). They allocate no carry ancilla and emit no CCX
  for low positions whose carry is identically 0.
- It has an extra A/B switch: `SUB4_EXACT_DEAD_LOW=0` turns it off.
- The five endpoint call sites in `pingpong_div.rs` switch over:
  `conditional_mod_negate` and both `seed_round_one*` (constant `f − 1`,
  whose bits 0–3 are clear), and `mod_halve_pm` and `mod_double_pm`
  (constant `f`, with the first carry proved 0 at the call site).
- This saves about 30 Toffoli exactly. See
  [adders-and-carries](../concepts/adders-and-carries.md).

### 4. Tail nonce (`src/point_add/mod.rs`)

`SUB4_PINGPONG_TAIL_NONCE` default 660000097677 (the base) → 21955808.

**Net.** About +1,500 Toffoli from the extra chunk repairs under the two
lower budgets, minus about 30 from dead-low, gives +1,469 measured, for
−2 qubits. The break-even for −2 qubits at this size is
2 × 939,026 / 1276 ≈ 1,472 Toffoli. So the trade clears break-even by only
a few Toffoli, which is why the score gain is so small.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **The budget changes are exact as layouts.** Each chunked add still
  computes the same sum. Only where the chunk boundaries fall, and how many
  there are, changes.
- **More truncated repairs mean more λ.** Every extra boundary adds one
  truncated compare. When its top bits of sum and addend tie, the repair
  leaves a wrong phase, which shows as phase garbage. So λ rises a little.
- **The dead-low elision is exact.**
- The shipped nonce `21955808` draws a clean test set: 0 classical, 0 phase
  and 0 ancilla failures. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The gain is a qubit-for-Toffoli trade, made by budgeting how many carry
ancillas may be live at once in both peak owners. Budget retunes are filed
as ancilla-lifetime / venting. The dead-low elision is a secondary exact
Toffoli offset.

   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures
