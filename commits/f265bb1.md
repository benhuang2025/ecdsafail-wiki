## Summary

The apply-step `+f` overflow folds each have a baked count `g` of clean,
measured carries. A knob adds two more to every fold with `g ≥ MIN_G`. This
commit lowers `MIN_G` from 18 to 16. The baked table has no entry at 17 and two
at 16, so exactly two folds go from `g = 16` to `g = 18`. Two more fold bits per
call get a cheap measured carry instead of the chunked suffix adder. With the
re-hunted nonce, the average falls by 8 Toffoli. Peak qubits stay at 1165.

## What changed and why it saves Toffoli

- `src/point_add/mod.rs`, `build()`: `LUD_EXTRA_FOLD_MIN_G` 18 → 16. `LUD_EXTRA_FOLD_VENTS` stays 2.
  - `load_schedule()` in `trailmix_ludicrous/mod.rs` maps each entry `x` of
    `schedule::FFG_G` (516 entries, one per apply cofactor add/sub) with `x ≥ MIN_G` to
    `min(x + 2, 53)`.
  - The values below 18 in the table are 9, 10, 11 (×2), 12 (×5), 13 (×3), 15 and 16 (×2).
    The new threshold therefore adds exactly the two `g = 16` entries, which become 18.
  - In `add_f_window` (`trailmix_ludicrous/arith.rs`), `g` is the length of the clean
    prefix of the 54-bit fold `reg[..54] += ctrl·f`. Each prefix bit has its own carry wire,
    which is erased by `hmr` plus `cz_if_bit`.
  - The remaining `54 − g` bits go through `controlled_add_const_chunked_graduated_off`.
    That adder's boundary-carry erases cost extra Toffoli. So moving two bits into the
    prefix saves Toffoli and uses two more wires during those two folds.
  - The author of [`ed2715f`](ed2715f.md) had kept `MIN_G` at 18 because the low-`g`
    entries sit at the apply's tightest headroom. On the 1165-qubit tree of
    [`cea9f5f`](cea9f5f.md), the two `g = 16` folds still fit under the peak.
- `DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE`: 100000033721 → 287. The comment above it is updated.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

The fold adder is exact for any `g`. Its existing approximation, which drops
the carry out of bit 53, is unchanged. The risk is the peak: two more live
wires during those two folds. The measured peak stays at 1165. The op stream
changed, so the nonce was re-hunted.

- The author scanned nonces 0 to 99,999 on a GPU prefilter.
- Nonce `287` then passed the CPU evaluator with 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla
  failures over all 9,024 shots.
- The author reports 10,662,471 emitted ops.

Part of the −8 may be draw-to-draw noise from the new nonce rather than the
two folds. See [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The commit tunes the vent schedule of a truncated fold adder, which is why the
census calls it fold/carry truncation. The truncation itself does not move.
The edit hands two more fold carries to measured vents, so **Register and
Scratch Allocation → ancilla-lifetime / venting** describes it more exactly.
The header keeps the published label.

   The base is the git parent. `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol cea9f5f f265bb1 -- src/point_add` is 12 changed lines in 1 file.

3. **Check the tree.** `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol f265bb1 -- src/point_add`
   should print nothing.
4. **Run the benchmark.** Run `./setup.sh && ./benchmark.sh` with no `DIALOG_*`, `TLM_*`, `LUD_*` or `CONSTPROP_*`
   environment variables set. Expect:
   - 1165 qubits
   - 1,413,479 average Toffoli (rounded; 1,413,479.340 before rounding)
   - score 1,646,703,035
   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures

The score only reproduces with the nonce `287` and an identical op stream.
