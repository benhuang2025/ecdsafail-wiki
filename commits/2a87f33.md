## Summary

This commit bundles several exact Toffoli savings and spends part of them on
lowering the failure risk. The peak stays at 1285.

1. **One-CCX majority in every constant-fold carry.** The truncated
   constant adds of the Solinas folds compute carries
   `maj(acc_i, ctrl, carry)` with 2 CCX (or 3). The identity
   `maj(a,k,c) = c ⊕ ((a⊕c)·(k⊕c))` needs only 1 CCX plus CX
   conjugation. This runs at every set constant position of every fold in
   every apply step.
2. **Two K2 overflow muxes** in the fused double/halve of `y` each lose one
   CCX, because the needed value can be rewritten from bits that are
   already live.
3. **Cheaper carry relief for the early GCD bodies.** [da398df](da398df.md)
   saved wires by streaming up to 7 high body bits per step through a
   controlled in-place adder, at about 6 extra Toffoli per bit, or about
   3 per wire saved. This commit streams only 2 bits per step. It
   recovers the other wires with "top-clean" bits: the top bits of the
   materialized prefix use coherent MAJ/UMA (no carry wire), at +1 Toffoli
   per wire saved.
4. **Spending.**
   - The GCD runs 260 steps instead of 258, which removes non-convergence
     risk.
   - Step 10 gets its full 256-bit body back, which removes that step's
     6-bit trim risk.
   - The final apply ripple gets 5 top-clean bits, which hold the apply
     phase at 1285.
   - The net average is −2,322 Toffoli. The shipped clean nonce is the
     small value `213`, which suggests an early hit if the search started
     at 0. The note does not say.

## What changed and why it saves Toffoli

1. **`DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_MAJ1=1`** (`src/point_add/arith/const_arith.rs`).
   - New `emit_fold_majority(a, k, carry, target, maj2)`. When `FOLD_MAJ1`
     is on and the four qubits are distinct, it emits
     `cx(carry,target); cx(carry,a); cx(carry,k); ccx(a,k,target); cx(carry,k); cx(carry,a)`.
     That is `target ^= carry ⊕ (a⊕carry)(k⊕carry) = maj(a,k,carry)` with
     one CCX. Otherwise it falls back to the old 2-CCX `maj2` or 3-CCX form.
   - It replaces the inline majority at set constant positions in
     `cadd/csub_nbit_const_direct_fast`, `cadd/csub_nbit_const_direct_trunc_fast`
     and `cadd/csub_per_position_controls_trunc`.
   - On this route (`DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_MAJ2=1`, `DIALOG_GCD_PERPOS_MAJ2=1`)
     that is −1 CCX per position:
     - in the K2 fused double/halve fold, 11 controlled positions with a
       carry-in (bits 1, 4–11, 32 and 33);
     - in the apply add/sub overflow fold, 6 positions (bits 4, 6–9 and 32
       of `c = 2^32 + 977`).

     That is about −17 per apply step, about −8,800 over 2 × 260 steps.
     Other `direct_fast` users go from 3 CCX to 1.
2. **K2 overflow muxes** (`rounds/dialog/compressed.rs`):
   - `dialog_gcd_fused_double_y`: `e = (ovf1 ∧ ¬s2) ⊕ ovf2` was one CCX.
     Since `d = ovf1 ∧ s2` is already computed, it equals `ovf1 ⊕ d ⊕ ovf2`,
     which needs three CX.
   - `dialog_gcd_fused_halve_y`: `ovf1 = s2 ? d : e` was two CCX. It is now
     `e ⊕ (s2 ∧ xed)`, using the already-live `xed = d ⊕ e`, which needs one
     CX and one CCX.
   - −1 CCX per call, about −520.
3. **Top-clean prefix bits in the streamed GCD body** (`rounds/dialog/mod.rs`,
   `compressed.rs`).
   - New `DIALOG_GCD_SELECTED_BODY_STREAM_TOPCLEAN_MAP`, read by
     `dialog_gcd_selected_body_stream_topclean_bits(step, prefix_len)` (map
     value or global `..._STREAM_TOPCLEAN`, capped at `prefix_len − 1`).
   - With `s ≥ 2` the body now calls
     `cuccaro_*_fast_prefix_ctrl_suffix_no_cin_topclean`. These were added
     unused in [b7515d5](b7515d5.md). The top `clean_top` prefix positions
     ripple with coherent MAJ/UMA, and only the low `prefix_len − clean_top`
     positions use measured carry wires.
   - Scratch demand becomes `2·prefix_len − clean_top + 1`. This applies to
     both the body (`nocin_need`) and `dialog_gcd_build_composite_scratch`.
   - Pins:
     - `..._STREAM_SUFFIX_MAP` goes from `3:2,4:3,5:5,...` to 2 at every
       step 3–17 and 19 (steps 18 and 21 stay at 1);
     - `..._STREAM_TOPCLEAN_MAP = 4:2,5:6,6:8,7:10,8:6,9:10,10:18,11:8,12:6,13:10,14:6,15:8,16:2,17:6,19:2`.
   - Each top-clean entry is `2 × (old s − 2)`, so it replaces the two
     wires per un-streamed bit, except at steps 11 and 12, where it is 2
     lower (8 and 6 instead of 10 and 8). Step 10 has an extra +12 for
     item 4.
   - Streamed bits fall from 82 to 32 per walk, at about −6 Toffoli each.
     There are 108 top-clean bits per walk, at +1 each. Over 4 walks that is
     about −1,200 + 430.
4. **`DIALOG_GCD_BODY_STEP_GIVEBACKS=10:6`**, read by the new
   `dialog_gcd_body_step_giveback(step)`. It subtracts 6 from the step-10 body
   trim `w`, cancelling its band trim 3 and binder notch 3. Step 10's body is
   full width again, which costs about +12 Toffoli per walk and 12 wires.
   The extra step-10 top-clean bits cover the wires.
5. **`DIALOG_GCD_ACTIVE_ITERATIONS`: 258 → 260.** This adds two GCD steps to
   both divisions: walk, reverse walk and apply. The added steps cost
   Toffoli, mainly in the full-width apply. In exchange, inputs that need
   259 or 260 steps no longer fail.
6. **`DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_FINAL_TOPCLEAN=5`** (`rounds/dialog/config.rs`). The
   final ripple of the apply chunked add/sub uses the new
   `cuccaro_add/sub_fast_low_to_ext_topclean` in `arith/adder.rs`, with 5
   coherent top positions. That saves 5 carry wires for +5 Toffoli per call,
   about +2,600. The author describes it as the same carry-relief trade.
   My reading is that it keeps the apply binders at 1285 under the longer
   run. I did not trace the peak.
7. **Dormant knob `DIALOG_GCD_WIDTH_STEP_BUMPS`** (per-step `active_width`
   bump, not set).
8. **Classical filter** (`dialog_gcd_classical_filter.rs`):
   - it mirrors `WIDTH_STEP_BUMPS` and `BODY_STEP_GIVEBACKS`;
   - a new option `DIALOG_GCD_FILTER_ACCEPT_U1_TERMINAL` accepts a factor
     whose truncated walk ends at `u = 1` within the iteration budget,
     instead of requiring full convergence.
9. **`DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE`: 4591773 → 213.**

Items 1–3 and 6 have exact per-call counts: about −10,100 for items 1–3,
and about −7,500 once item 6's +2,600 is included. Items 4 and 5 give back
the rest (about +5,200) of the difference to the measured −2,322. I did not
separately count the cost of the two extra GCD steps.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

Items 1–3 and 6 are exact rewrites:
- the one-CCX majority identity is exact for all inputs, and its CX
  conjugation restores `a` and `k`;
- the mux rewrites follow from `d = ovf1 ∧ s2` and `xed = d ⊕ e`;
- top-clean prefix bits and the top-clean final ripple compute the same sums
  with coherent instead of measured carry uncompute.

Items 4 and 5 lower the failure risk. Step 10 no longer truncates its body,
and the GCD has two more steps to converge. Lambda therefore goes down,
which is consistent with the small nonce value. The remaining
truncations (other body notches, comparators, fold windows) are unchanged.
A failure would show up as classical mismatches and ancilla or phase
garbage.

The shipped nonce `213` draws a test set with 0 classical, 0 phase and 0
ancilla failures over all 9,024 shots. See
[nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The main Toffoli levers change gate or adder forms:
- a cheaper majority circuit in the fold adders;
- coherent top-clean ripples replacing streamed controlled adders for wire
  relief.

That fits Strength Reduction → fast-adder reroute. The two extra GCD steps
and the step-10 giveback are not savings. They spend the saved Toffoli on a
lower Lambda.

   372 lines in 7 files.

   The flag lets the patch apply whether the files use LF or CRLF line
   endings. It is the complete diff against the base.
3. **Check the tree.** `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol 2a87f33 -- src/point_add`
   should print nothing.
4. **Run the benchmark.** Run `./setup.sh && ./benchmark.sh` with no
   `DIALOG_*`, `KAL_*`, `MOD_*`, `R84_*` or `ROUND84_*` environment variables
   set. Expect:
   - 1285 qubits
   - 1,382,662 average Toffoli (1,382,661.672 before rounding)
   - score 1,776,720,670
   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures

The score only reproduces with the nonce `213` and an identical op stream.
Any other change needs a new nonce hunt.
