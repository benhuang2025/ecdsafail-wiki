## Summary

On 23 August two trees grew from gnuchev's `4eb93cb`:

- moscowchill's [`51c6c31`](51c6c31.md) parked temporaries in live wires and
  went to 1267 qubits, at higher Toffoli.
- mochimodev's own [`d919bc6`](d919bc6.md) cut 5,442 Toffoli at 1273 qubits.

This commit merges the first into the second. Its diff against `d919bc6` is
essentially `51c6c31`'s diff against `4eb93cb`, adapted to the new code, plus
a retune of the pins.

One choice was needed to reach 1267. `d919bc6`'s signed replay frame keeps
more wires live in the divide replay cell than the canonical cell does once
the canonical cell has `51c6c31`'s parking. So the frame is turned off
(`SUB4_PP_SIGNED_FRAME=0`), which the in-code comment prices at about +628
Toffoli. The result has 6 fewer qubits and 2,589 more Toffoli than `d919bc6`.
It beats both parents: 1,154,731,130 against 1,156,903,673 (`d919bc6`) and
1,161,351,205 (`51c6c31`).

## What changed and why it saves qubits

### 1. Ported from 51c6c31 unchanged

These hunks are byte-for-byte the same as in `51c6c31`. Their mechanisms are
explained in sections 1 to 5 of the [`51c6c31`](51c6c31.md) note.
`git diff 51c6c31 6752417` shows no change at all in the last four files
below. In `mod.rs`, only the pins and the nonce differ.

- `mod.rs`: the `release_clean` builder method.
- `arith/compare.rs`: the phase-only top of `cmp_lt_phase_conditioned`.
  - No `c_in` wire, `n − 1` carries, and a 3-CZ majority phase.
  - This replaces `d919bc6`'s own version, which kept `c_in` and used
    `Z·CZ`. It saves one more wire per boundary repair, and the Toffoli is
    the same.
  - Also `cmp_lt_phase_conditioned_with_cin_borrowed_carries`.
- `arith/const_arith.rs`: `csub_nbit_const_direct_trunc_fast_dead_low_ctrl_low0_host`.
  `mod_halve_pm`'s final borrow is hosted in `target[0]`.
- `trailmix_ludicrous/arith.rs` and `comparator.rs`:
  `compare_geq_chunked_middle_complement_phase`, so the trailmix modular
  add/sub needs no `lt_flag` wire.

### 2. Ported into d919bc6's pingpong_div.rs, with merge resolutions

- **Terminal carry into the output.** In `fused_fold_maskfree` this is
  unchanged from `51c6c31`: `num_carries = width − 3`, and the operand is held
  in `selectors[0]`. The two walk adders needed merging:
  - `signed_add_wrapping_sigma`. `d919bc6` had already dropped two carry
    wires (`n − 3`). The direct terminal stage drops one more, so it is now
    `n − 4` whenever `n ≥ 5`, and the loop stops at `n − 2`.
    - When the (off-by-default) `top_skip` applies, the last two sum bits
      close over the same carry with no CCX.
    - Otherwise the last carry is CCXed straight into `target[n−1]`.
  - `signed_add_wrapping_sigma_split`. The high chunk always keeps
    `high − 1` carries (`high_loop_end = high − 1`), with the same two
    terminal cases.
- **Split-boundary compare.** As in `51c6c31`, it starts at bit 2 with
  `source[1]` as the carry-in, and its top is a 3-CZ phase. The unit test
  `sigma_split_low_two_compare_reduction_is_exact` comes with it.
- **Replay-cell parking.**
  - `signed_mod_add_pm_halve_fused` (canonical divide cell): `plus_f` is held
    in `parity`, and `sign_and_parity` is freed and rebuilt.
  - `signed_mod_double_add_pm_fused` (multiply cell): `plus_f` is held in
    `add_out`, and `odd_correction` and `sign_xor_add` are released and
    rebuilt.
  - `d919bc6`'s `Option` for `plus_2f` is kept, and the canonical cells pass
    `Some(plus_2f)`.
- **Plumbing.**
  - `plan(direction, rounds)` reads `SUB4_PP_R1_MUL` for the multiply
    traversal.
  - `walk_peak()` reads `SUB4_PP_WALK_PEAK`.
  - `replay_fold_window_mul()` and `replay_fold_target_mul()` are added.
  - The multiply cell's fold uses the `_MUL` window.
- **Width repair back on.** `width_repair()` loses the `if false &&`, so the
  100 sparse +1-bit rounds return: about +519 Toffoli and −0.95 Lambda (see
  [`4eb93cb`](4eb93cb.md)).

### 3. Ported into d919bc6's square (`square/product_register.rs`)

- **Karatsuba sum in place.** `square_sub` no longer builds a 129-wire `sum`
  register at the start. After branch B it adds `y_lo` into `y[h..]` plus one
  `sum_carry` wire. It then runs `d919bc6`'s level-2 `karatsuba_branch` on
  that in-place sum, and subtracts `y_lo` back. That frees 128 wires during
  the square. `karatsuba_branch` still builds its own `t = a + b` register of
  65 or 66 wires.
- `SQUARE_CHUNK_MIN = usize::MAX`: exact full-width square adds, as in
  `51c6c31`. `SUB4_SQUARE_CHUNK_MIN=200` restores the chunked path.
- The `tri_corr` single-subtraction correction applies to every
  `tri_square`, including the Karatsuba-2 sub-squares.
- The phase-only `clear_overflow_phase` is ported too.
- `apply_shifted_full_term` and the rotated `apply_shift_half`. With
  Karatsuba-2 on, branches A and C fold through `fold_shifted`, so these now
  only affect branch B (`y_hi`).

- **`SUB4_PP_SIGNED_FRAME=0`.** The divide replay goes back to the canonical
  cell, which now has the parking.
  - The comment reads: "the level-2 Karatsuba square (ours) needs 6 more live
    wires in the replay cell than the canonical-residue frame does … Canonical
    residues cost ~628 T and buy the peak down to 1267 — 105 T/wire against a
    ~716 break-even."
  - Taken literally, the first sentence is garbled, because the square is not
    in the replay cell. What the code does is drop the signed cell, whose
    extra `hi` wire and unparked `plus_f` keep it wider. The cost is giving
    up the signed frame's saving: −1,181 Toffoli in `d919bc6`'s comment
    (measured on an earlier tree), about 628 by this commit's comment.
- **`ROUNDS_MUL` 694.** Two multiply rounds fewer than `d919bc6`. That saves
  two rounds of walk, walk-back and replay Toffoli and two tape wires in the
  multiply replay, and it raises the multiply walk's non-convergence Lambda.
- **`ENDPOINT_FOLD_WINDOW` 26.** With LOW56 on, the endpoint fold chains stay
  at 52 (divide) and 51 (multiply), as explained in the `51c6c31` note. But
  `d919bc6`'s `round1_window = 32 + endpoint` grows from 52 to 58 positions.
  So the fused round-1 borrow is dropped less often, for about 6 more
  Toffoli per execution.
- **Tail nonce:** 5020638865 → 8107117281543.

### Where the qubits and Toffoli come from

- **Qubits (−6).** The replay and walk cells lose the parked temporaries,
  the terminal carry wires, the compare's `c_in` and top carry, and the
  signed cell's extra wire. `PEAK` and `WALK_PEAK` drop by 6, so the
  ladders do not take that room back. The square frees 128 wires and spends
  them on exact full-width adds.
- **Toffoli (+2,589).**
  - Adds Toffoli: the signed frame off (about +628), the chunk compare
    21 → 22 (about +1,100 by `d919bc6`'s price), the width repair back on
    (about +519), the wider round-1 window, and more chunks under a 6-wire
    smaller ladder budget.
  - Saves Toffoli: two fewer multiply rounds, the shorter split compare,
    `tri_corr`, the branch-B rotation, the exact square adds and
    `FOLD_WINDOW_MUL` 53.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- Every ported parking and phase-only change is exact, for the reasons given
  in the [`51c6c31`](51c6c31.md) note. The merge resolutions in the walk
  adders only combine two exact terminal forms.
- Turning the signed frame off removes its truncated parity repair and frame
  conversions. In their place it restores the canonical cell's flag compare
  (`REPLAY_FLAG_COMPARE` = 22), so the truncation exposure is about the same.
- The Lambda-moving knobs:
  - Lower Lambda: the chunk compare 22, the width repair, and the wider
    round-1 window.
  - Higher Lambda: two fewer multiply rounds, `FOLD_WINDOW_MUL` 53, and more
    boundary repairs from the tighter ladders.
- The shipped nonce draws a clean test set: 9,024 of 9,024 shots, with 0
  classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

Against its base, the whole score gain is the 6-qubit drop, bought by reusing
live wires as scratch, while Toffoli goes up. That is scratch/ancilla reuse,
the same mechanism as [`51c6c31`](51c6c31.md), applied to a cheaper tree. The
signed-frame switch-off is a trade made to let the parking reach the peak,
not a separate lever.

## Reproduce

1. **Start from the base.** Clone https://github.com/ecdsafail/ecdsafail-challenge
   and run `git checkout d919bc6 -- src/point_add`.
   - The base is the git parent. `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol d919bc6 6752417 -- src/point_add`
     is 855 changed lines (652 added, 203 removed) in 7 files.
   - The other parent of the merge, `51c6c31`, would also work as a base. The
     diff is then 1,052 lines in 3 files, because it re-adds all of
     `d919bc6`'s levers. Diffing against `d919bc6` isolates what the merge
     itself adds.
2. **Apply the patch below** from the repo root with
   `git apply --ignore-whitespace`. The flag lets the patch apply whether the
   files use LF or CRLF line endings. It is the complete diff against the
   base.
3. **Check the tree.** `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol 6752417 -- src/point_add`
   should print nothing.
4. **Run the benchmark.** Run `./setup.sh && ./benchmark.sh` with no `SUB4_*`
   or `TLM_*` environment variables set. The pins are applied by
   `set_default_env` inside `build()`, and an outside value would override
   them. Expect:
   - 1267 qubits
   - 911,390 average Toffoli (rounded)
   - score 1,154,731,130
   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures

The score only reproduces with the nonce `8107117281543` and an identical op
stream. Any other change needs a new nonce hunt.
