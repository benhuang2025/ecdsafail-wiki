## Summary

odinfree's [`7689eac`](7689eac.md) reached 1226 qubits from an older tree
(`98dd2ad`), so it lacked several exact Toffoli savings that had landed on the
1285-qubit frontier in the meantime. This commit ports them across and keeps
the 1226-qubit peak. The ported pieces are:

- the fold majority gate in 1 Toffoli instead of 2;
- the zero-pair swap skips of [`b8ce940`](b8ce940.md) and [`7822c37`](7822c37.md);
- one Toffoli less in each fused double and halve;
- a signed-digit (NAF) product `quotient · c` in the round84 square, with
  measured instead of coherent adders for its small adds;
- a "top-clean" final apply chunk, which costs Toffoli.

The net is −10,161 Toffoli. All the circuit changes are exact. The nonce was
re-hunted because the op stream changed.

## What changed and why it saves Toffoli

### Active changes

1. **1-CCX fold majority (`DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_MAJ1=1`, `arith/const_arith.rs`).**
   - New `emit_fold_majority(b, a, k, carry, target, maj2)` is used by every
     constant-fold carry sweep:
     - `cadd/csub_nbit_const_direct_fast`;
     - `cadd/csub_nbit_const_direct_trunc_fast` (the apply add/sub fold);
     - `cadd/csub_per_position_controls_trunc` (the fused double/halve fold).
   - When `DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_MAJ1=1` and the four wires are distinct, it emits
     `emit_fold_maj1`:
     `cx(carry, target); cx(carry, a); cx(carry, k); ccx(a, k, target); cx(carry, k); cx(carry, a)`.
   - This uses `maj(a, k, c) = c ⊕ (a⊕c)(k⊕c)`, so the carry costs one CCX.
     The MAJ2 form already on the route costs two, and the plain form three.
   - The fold constant `c = 2^32 + 977` has set bits {0, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 32}. So
     each apply add/sub fold has 6 majority positions, and each fused
     double/halve has 11 controlled positions with a carry-in. Over 258 steps ×
     2 apply passes, that is about 516 × (6 + 11) ≈ 8,800 Toffoli, the largest
     piece. See [modular-fold](../concepts/modular-fold.md).
2. **Zero-pair swap skips (`DIALOG_GCD_SKIP_ZERO_EDGE_CSHIFT=1`).**
   - The seven switches and the four `continue`s in
     `rounds/dialog/compressed.rs` are copied from [`b8ce940`](b8ce940.md) and
     [`7822c37`](7822c37.md), and the master switch is pinned on.
   - Each skipped `cswap` exchanges two wires that are always 0. That is one
     Toffoli per walk step in four walk passes, and one per apply double or
     halve: about 4 × 258 + 2 × 258 ≈ 1,550.
3. **One CCX less in the fused double and halve (`compressed.rs`).**
   - In `dialog_gcd_fused_double_y`, `e = (ovf1 & ¬s2) ⊕ ovf2` was
     `x(s2); ccx(ovf1, s2, e); x(s2); cx(ovf2, e)`. Since `d = ovf1 & s2` is
     already computed, `ovf1 & ¬s2 = ovf1 ⊕ d`, so it becomes three CNOTs.
   - In `dialog_gcd_fused_halve_y`, `ovf1 = s2 ? d : e` was two CCX. It becomes
     `cx(e, ovf1); ccx(s2, xed, ovf1)`, using the live `xed = d ⊕ e`.
   - This saves one CCX per call, about 516.
4. **NAF quotient product (`R84_QPROD_NAF=1`, `arith/multiply.rs`).**
   - The round84 in-place Solinas square accumulates a 33-bit quotient `q`.
     `round84_compute_quotient_c_product` then forms `q · c`.
   - Before, it did 6 shifted adds of `q`, at shifts {4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 32}, from
     977 = 1 + 2^4 + 2^6 + 2^7 + 2^8 + 2^9. With NAF it does 4: +2^10, +2^32,
     −2^5, −2^4, from 977 = 2^10 − 2^5 − 2^4 + 1.
   - `round84_uncompute_quotient_c_product` mirrors this in reverse order.
5. **Measured small adders in the round84 fold (`ROUND84_FOLD_FAST_ADD=1`).**
   - New `round84_add_small` / `round84_sub_small` use `add/sub_nbit_qq_fast`
     (about 1 CCX per bit, with a carry register erased by measurement)
     instead of the coherent Cuccaro `add/sub_nbit_qq` (2 CCX per bit, no
     carry register).
   - They are used for the quotient-product adds and for the narrow correction
     add/subtract (`round84_add/sub_narrow_correction`).
   - The comment says this saves 1,434 executed Toffoli. It is peak-neutral
     because these adders are at most 66 bits wide and run in a low-occupancy
     phase.
6. **Top-clean final apply chunk (`DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_FINAL_TOPCLEAN=5`).**
   - New `cuccaro_add/sub_fast_low_to_ext_topclean(..., clean_top)` in
     `arith/adder.rs` runs a measured-carry ripple on the low `n − 5` bits and
     an in-place MAJ/UMA ripple on the top 5 bits.
   - It is used for the final chunk of `dialog_gcd_add/sub_ctrl_chunked_low_to_ext`
     (`rounds/dialog/mod.rs`), with the switch read by
     `dialog_gcd_apply_final_topclean_bits()` in `config.rs`.
   - This saves 5 carry wires in that chunk, but costs about one extra CCX per
     top bit, so about +2,600 over 516 calls. The commit does not say why it
     was kept. The peak stays at 1226.
7. **`DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE`**: 9100016331678 → 2000004892833. A stale comment
   about windowing the apply final ripple into 2 blocks is deleted.

These add up to about −8,800 − 1,550 − 516 − (NAF + 1,434) + 2,600, which is
close to the measured −10,161. The split is an estimate.

The route keeps `DIALOG_GCD_RAW_TOBITVECTOR_MATERIALIZED_SUB=0` (the vented

`dialog_gcd_width_step_bump` is live code, but `DIALOG_GCD_WIDTH_STEP_BUMPS` is

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **All six active changes are exact identities.**
  - The 1-CCX majority computes the same carry bit. The temporary XORs into
    `a` and `k` are undone before anything else reads them.
  - The skipped swaps act on known-zero wires.
  - `ovf1 ⊕ d` equals `ovf1 & ¬s2` because `d = ovf1 & s2`.
  - The NAF product is the same integer `q · 977` plus `q · 2^32`. It only
    needs the target register to absorb the intermediate negative terms
    modulo its width, and the uncompute mirrors it exactly.
  - Fast adders and top-clean ripples compute the same sums as the coherent
    ones. Their carries are erased by `hmr` + `cz_if`, which cancels the phase
    exactly ([measurement-uncompute](../concepts/measurement-uncompute.md)).
- **Probabilistic parts.** None are added. The inherited ones (width envelope,
  258 steps, compare cap 46, carry windows 19/18, apply clean 19) are
  unchanged, but the new op stream draws a new test set.
- The shipped nonce `2000004892833` draws a clean set: 9,024 of 9,024 shots,
  with 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. A wrong identity would
  show as phase garbage (for the measured uncomputes) or as a classical
  mismatch. See [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The published label is Uncompute Strategy → measurement uncompute. That fits
`ROUND84_FOLD_FAST_ADD`, which turns coherent adders into measured ones.
However, that is only about 1,400 of the 10,161. By the estimate above, the
largest pieces are:

- the 1-CCX majority in the fold adders (about 8,800), which is a cheaper gate
  identity for the Solinas fold;
- the NAF recoding of `c`.

Both are Strength Reduction → solinas / NAF fold. The zero-pair skips are
dead-gate removal. As a whole the commit is a forward-port bundle, and
Strength Reduction → solinas / NAF fold describes its main saving better than
measurement uncompute.

   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures
