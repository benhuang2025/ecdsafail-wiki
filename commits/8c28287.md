## Summary

Each dialog-GCD step only works on the low `active_width` bits of `u` and
`v`. That width follows a straight-line envelope, `256 − step × slope`, plus a
safety margin. This commit lowers the margin from 10 to 9 bits, which
narrows every step's window, except the first few that are clamped at 256.

Narrower windows make every step's cswap and controlled subtract cheaper in
all GCD passes. The saving is 4,128 average Toffoli at the same 1320 qubits.
The cost is more inputs whose numbers have not shrunk enough and get
truncated. The author estimates the clean nonce density at about 1 in
260,000 and found nonce 700017357 with a classical pre-filter.

## What changed and why it saves Toffoli

Both changes are default pins in `configure_ecdsafail_submission_route()`,
`src/point_add/mod.rs`.

1. **`DIALOG_GCD_WIDTH_MARGIN`: 10 → 9.**
   - `dialog_gcd_width_margin()` reads this value. It feeds
     `dialog_gcd_tobitvector_active_width(step) = ceil_even(256 − step × slope + margin)`,
     clamped to `[1, 256]`. On this tree the slope is 1.005.
   - Steps up to about 9 stay clamped at 256. After that, one bit less margin
     makes about half of the steps 2 bits narrower once rounded to even. The
     rest are unchanged.
   - The window sets the width of the step's cswap of `u` and `v` (one CCX per
     bit), the gated load and ripple of its controlled subtract (about two
     CCX per bit, see `dialog_gcd_controlled_sub_selected`), and, on late steps
     where the window is under 46 bits, the branch comparator.
   - The −4,128 is summed over the forward and reverse transcript passes of
     both GCD uses. That is about 16 Toffoli per step.
2. **`DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE`: 60003453 → 700017357.** The 48 `X;X` identity
   pairs at the end of `build_builder()` only reseed the 9,024 test inputs.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- The envelope assumes that after `s` steps both `u` and `v` are shorter than
  `256 − s × slope + margin` bits. The margin is the safety gap. An input
  that shrinks more slowly keeps a 1 above the window, and that bit is then
  ignored by the cswap and subtract. The GCD result is wrong, which gives a
  classical failure, and uncomputing the corrupted transcript also gives
  phase garbage. The author notes that margin 9 adds such "phase residue".
- The author's comment calls this "value-exact on the reachable support".
  It is not: the cut adds hard inputs, which is why the island is sparse.
- The pre-filter derives each nonce's 9,024 inputs. It replays the truncated
  GCD classically on both factors (`dx = Px − Qx` and `c = Qx − Rx`) and
  rejects inputs that overflow the envelope, do not converge, or mis-decide a
  branch. The survivors are fully simulated.
- Nonce 700017357 draws a clean set: 9,024 of 9,024 shots, with 0 classical,
  0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

A smaller width margin narrows the GCD register window, which is
comparator/width truncation. No runner-up fits.

## Reproduce

1. **Start from the base.** Clone https://github.com/ecdsafail/ecdsafail-challenge
   and run `git checkout 5ad23b1 -- src/point_add`.
   - Do not start from the git parent `8a9d274`. That is factory-sagar's tree,
     from the mpjunior92 / newjordan / robertkodra line.
   - Diffing against the parent (82 lines) would wrongly show this commit
     moving the slope 1010 → 1005, the late band trims 2 → 1,
     `KAL_FOLD_CARRY_TRUNC_W` 22 → 23, the apply clean compare 19 → 18 and
     `DIALOG_REROLL` 506906 → 4269. Those are only the differences between
     the two lines.
   - Against `5ad23b1`, `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol 5ad23b1 8c28287 -- src/point_add`
     is 18 lines in 1 file.
2. **Apply the patch below** from the repo root with `git apply --ignore-whitespace`.
   The flag lets the patch apply whether the files use LF or CRLF line endings.
   It is the complete diff against the base.
3. **Check the tree.** `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol 8c28287 -- src/point_add`
   should print nothing.
4. **Run the benchmark.** Run `./setup.sh && ./benchmark.sh` with no
   `KAL_*`, `DIALOG_*` or `SUB4_*` environment variables set, because they
   override the defaults. Expect:
   - 1320 qubits
   - 1,550,891 average Toffoli (rounded)
   - score 2,047,176,120
   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures

The score only reproduces with the nonce `700017357` and an identical op
stream. Any other change needs a new nonce hunt.
