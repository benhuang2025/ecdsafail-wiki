## Summary

At 1170 qubits several phases of the dialog-GCD route sat at the peak
together:
- the coefficient "apply" phase, which replays the packed GCD transcript;
- its chunked carry ripple;
- the GCD-step fold;
- the windowed square.

This commit takes two qubits off every one of them.
- **Apply.** It decodes the K5 transcript blocks a pair or a slot at a time,
  instead of unpacking the whole block for the whole apply.
- **Carries.** It measures out a fold carry and the apply boundary carries
  earlier and recomputes them later. Where the chunked ripple would still
  exceed 1168, it hosts its top carries in place.
- **Square.** It windows two more square rows.
- **Last tail step.** It treats the very last GCD step as having a fixed shape
  ("s2 constant"), which skips that step's swap. This is exact only on the
  dominant 25 of the 32 reachable tail patterns.

Together this costs 1,140 average Toffoli and saves 2 qubits, a net score gain
of 1,533,552.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

Background (see [transcript-codecs](../concepts/transcript-codecs.md)): the dialog GCD runs 258 steps. Each
step's control bits (branch bits `b0`, `b1` and the second-shift bit `s2`) form
a small raw word. The raw words are packed in blocks:
- a first "head11" block with an 11-bit code;
- 50 ordinary K5 blocks of 5 steps, a 15-wire raw block packed into a 12-bit
  code;
- a final 3-step "tail3" block whose 9 raw bits are stored as a 5-bit "TOP32"
  code.

The apply phase walks the blocks back, decompresses each one and, step by
step, doubles or halves `y`, does a controlled modular add/subtract, and
swaps `x` and `y` under `b0∧b1`. Before this commit, a block's raw word was
fully decompressed for its whole apply.

### `rounds/dialog/compressed.rs` (copied, 833 diff lines)

- **Pair-streamed K5 apply** (`DIALOG_GCD_K5_STREAM_PAIR_APPLY = 1`) and the
  head11 variant (`DIALOG_GCD_K5_HEAD11_STREAM_PAIR_APPLY = 1`):
  - The block is decompressed only to the post-compressor "data" form
    (`dialog_gcd_k5_decompress_block_to_data`,
    `dialog_gcd_k5_head11_decompress_block_to_data`).
  - Its two pair-encoded slot pairs stay encoded, and the drop lanes
    `raw[0]`, `raw[4]` are freed (`dialog_gcd_k5_stream_pairs_start`).
  - Each pair is opened with `emit_dialog_gcd_k5_pair_encoder_inverse` just
    before the slots that need it, and re-encoded and freed right after
    (`..._stream_pairs_before_slot` and `..._after_slot_forward` / `_reverse`).
  - At block end the block is recompressed with
    `dialog_gcd_k5_compress_data_to_block`.
  - This saves raw lanes during most of each block's apply, at the price of
    the extra pair-encoder CCX.
- **Head11 shift without opening a pair:**
  - `DIALOG_GCD_K5_HEAD11_SPLIT_PAIR_SHIFT_APPLY = 1` opens a pair only for
    the add and swap of a slot.
  - `..._PAIR01_S2_PERMUTE_APPLY = 1` exposes slot 0's `s2` with a 2-CX +
    1-CCX permutation (`dialog_gcd_k5_head11_pair01_expose_s2`).
  - `..._PAIR23_S2_BORROW_PAIR01_APPLY = 1` computes slot 2's `s2` from the
    still-encoded pair 23 into a zeroed lane of pair 01 with a 9-term ANF
    (`DIALOG_GCD_K5_HEAD11_PAIR23_S2_ANF`, `..._toggle_pair23_s2_into`).
- **Split-slot tail3 apply** (`DIALOG_GCD_K5_TAIL3_TOP32_SPLIT_SLOT_APPLY = 1`).
  For each tail slot, only the shift bit is decoded from the 5-bit code for
  the double/halve (`..._toggle_slot_shift_from_code`). It is cleared before
  the two branch bits are decoded for the add and the swap
  (`..._toggle_slot_branch_from_code`). This keeps 1 raw bit live instead of 3.
- **Final step with constant s2** (`DIALOG_GCD_K5_TAIL3_TOP32_FINAL_S2_CONST_APPLY = 1`).
  This is the probabilistic part.
  - A second codec, `DIALOG_GCD_K5_TAIL3_TOP32_S2CONST_{CODE_CONSTANT = 3, ENCODER_ANF, DECODER_ANF}`,
    replaces the TOP32 maps everywhere: compress, decompress and the slot
    decodes.
  - Its decoder fixes the last slot's raw bit 5 (`b1`) to 0 and bit 8 (`s2`)
    to 1.
  - In that last step the apply does `dialog_gcd_fixed_double_twice_y` (or
    `_halve_twice_y` in reverse) with no control, and skips the `x`/`y`
    cswap, since `b0∧b1 = 0`. Only `b0` is decoded, for the add.
  - Ported to Python, the new maps are a bijection on the 25 support patterns
    whose last digit is 4 or 5 (`s2 = 1`, `b1 = 0`). The other 7 patterns
    (`0x4b, 0x49, 0x58, 0xc5, 0xc7, 0x38, 0x2b`) do not round-trip.
  - Counted with the dirty-ancilla cost 1/4/10/22 CCX per degree-2/3/4/5
    monomial, the decoder is also cheaper: 155 against 287 CCX for a full
    decode.
- **K5 partial raw release** (`mod.rs`): `DIALOG_GCD_K5_PARTIAL_RAW_RELEASE`
  6 → 8. It caps how many spare raw-block lanes a non-streamed partial
  block frees during its apply. The only partial block is the tail3 block,
  which is streamed, so the change appears inert (as in
  [`674d0d8`](674d0d8.md)).

### Fold carries (`arith/const_arith.rs`, `mod.rs`)

- `fold_ripple_freed_tail_ed_streamed()` is the fused pseudo-Mersenne fold of
  the GCD-step double/halve, with a 33-bit low carry chain `low[..]`. New flag
  `DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_FREE_FIRST_HIGH_CARRY = 1`:
  - right after the low ripple, the first unparked carry `low[park_low]` is
    measured out (`hmr` plus `fold_postsum_carry_phase_uncompute`) and freed;
  - it is reacquired and recomputed with `fold_postsum_carry_compute` before
    the final carry clears.
  - That is one qubit fewer during the truncated high-window ripple, paid for
    with an extra recompute. See
    [venting-and-wire-loans](../concepts/venting-and-wire-loans.md) and
    [modular-fold](../concepts/modular-fold.md).
- `DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_HOST_E_TOP_CARRY = 1` hosts the top low-chain carry on the
  fold control `e` (`low_chain_last = hi_delta − 1`), so one carry lane fewer
  is allocated.
- `DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_STREAM_CONTROLS = 1` turns on the streamed fold controls.
- Window settings:
  - A new first line sets `DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_CARRY_TRUNC_W = "18"`. The old
    in-block value becomes `"17"`, but the first line wins, so the global
    window stays 18.
  - `DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_CARRY_TRUNC_STEP_WINDOWS` is set to `""`. That drops all
    51 per-step 19- and 20-bit windows, so those steps narrow to 18 bits.
  - `DIALOG_GCD_SPECIAL_FOLD_PARK_LOW_CARRIES` goes 13 → 16, and its step map
    is cleared (it was 14 or 15 at 16 steps).

### Apply chunk ripple and boundary carries (`rounds/dialog/mod.rs`, `arith/adder.rs`, `config.rs`)

- **Auto top-clean.**
  - New `dialog_gcd_apply_auto_topclean_bits()`. Before each chunk ripple of
    `dialog_gcd_add_ctrl_chunked_low_to_ext` / `_sub_`, it projects the peak
    as `active_qubits + owned carries`.
  - If that exceeds `DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_CHUNKED_F_AUTO_TOPCLEAN_TARGET = 1168`,
    it uses up to `..._MAX_BITS = 2` "top-clean" bits.
  - The new `cuccaro_{add,sub}_fast_low_to_ext_borrowed_carries_topclean`
    (adder.rs) do those top positions as in-place Cuccaro MAJ/UMA, with no
    carry lane. That uncompute costs Toffoli instead of an `hmr`. The low
    positions keep borrowed carries with measured uncompute. See
    [adders-and-carries](../concepts/adders-and-carries.md).
  - The final chunk takes the max of this and the old
    `dialog_gcd_apply_final_topclean_bits()`.
- **Free during replay.** With
  `DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_BOUNDARY_FREE_OWNED_DURING_REPLAY = 1`, the new
  `dialog_gcd_conditional_boundary_replay_free_owned` frees each owned
  boundary carry right after its `hmr`, before its phase-conditioned compare.
  Before, all were freed after the last compare. See
  [measurement-uncompute](../concepts/measurement-uncompute.md).

### Square (`mod.rs`)

- `SQUARE_ROW_MAX_SEG` 143 → 141. Rows 114 and 115 (widths 143 and 142) are
  now also built in two windows by `square_row_windowed_apply`. That lowers
  their transient row register by about 70 qubits, but adds a boundary carry
  cleanup compare in each direction (see [`6878ef8`](6878ef8.md)).

### Other `mod.rs` edits

- The `DIALOG_FUSE_*` flags move up next to the other dialog flags.
- The `DIALOG_FUSE_SELFTEST` hook in `build()` is removed.
  `dialog_fuse_primitive_selftest()` stays in `arith/modular.rs`, unused.
- Comments are stripped from the fusion code.
- `DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE` → `"18100027017098"` (all three lines).

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **Exact.** These parts compute the same values as before:
  - the pair streaming, split-slot decode, s2 expose and borrow;
  - partial raw release;
  - early freeing of boundary carries;
  - top-clean ripples;
  - the fold carry free and recompute;
  - top-carry hosting.

  They only change when bits are decoded or which wire holds a carry. A
  mistake there would show up as ancilla garbage.
- **Square.** The extra square windows are exact for the product. Their
  18-bit boundary cleanups are approximate in the usual way: a tie in the top
  bits gives a phase error.
- **New risks.**
  - *The s2-constant tail.* An input whose last GCD step has `s2 = 0` or
    `b1 = 1` is mis-encoded by the new codec, and that last step is applied
    wrongly. This gives a classical failure, plus ancilla garbage when the code
    is un-computed. The submission note calls it "the dominant s2-constant
    case".
  - *Narrower fold windows.* Removing the 51 per-step 19- and 20-bit fold
    windows narrows those steps to 18 bits. More carries can escape, which
    gives classical failures. The note says the fold carry handling was tuned
    "to keep the circuit value-correct on the hunted island".
- **Nonce.** Nonce `18100027017098` draws a test set that avoids all of these:
  0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures over 9,024 shots. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The published label is Codec / Encoding → trailmix / K5 codec. Most of the
diff is in the K5 transcript codec's lifecycle: when pairs and slots are
decoded, plus a specialized tail codec. That fits. The goal and effect,
though, is a peak cut bought with Toffoli, by freeing wires early and
recomputing later across four co-binding phases. A peak-reduction / qubit
recycling label would have been a close runner-up.
