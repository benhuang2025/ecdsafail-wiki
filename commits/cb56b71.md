## Summary

In the base, the ping-pong divider runs the whole value walk first, then
replays all 700 rounds on the coefficient pair, then walks back. Every replay
round therefore runs with the full 700-qubit tape and both 256-bit
coefficient registers live, plus that round's carry scratch (a chunked-add
ladder of about 87 wires). That flat plateau is the 1316-qubit peak.

This commit replays each late round right after its own walk round (divide),
or right before its own walk-back round (multiply). A replay round `r` then
shares the machine only with `r + 1` tape bits and the walk registers at
width `value_width(r)`. Each round gets a scratch budget equal to what is left
under a target peak, `SUB4_PP_QSTAR = 1283`, and switches to narrower
arithmetic when that budget is small. Only the last ~200 rounds of each
traversal need the narrow arithmetic, so only they pay for it. The peak drops
by 35 qubits to 1281. The cost is +10,733 Toffoli, because the narrow rounds
need more truncated boundary repairs. The score falls 1.5%.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

All of the mechanism is in `src/point_add/pingpong_div.rs` (+1,000 / −42
lines, copied in Tier B). Two small edits are in the patch.

### 1. The fused schedule (`fused_div_mul_core`)

`pingpong_mod_mul_div_in_place` now dispatches on `fused_mode()`
(`SUB4_PP_FUSED`, default 1):

- `legacy_div_mul_core` is the old three-phase order. `SUB4_PP_FUSED=0`
  restores it.
- `fused_div_mul_core` is the new default.
- The even-lift fix-up, `restore_wire_layout` and the final frees move out of
  both cores into the caller.

The walk and replay are split into per-round functions: `walk_round`,
`walk_back_round`, `walk_back_finish`, `replay_halving_round` and
`replay_doubling_inverse_round`. Called with no budget, they emit the same
gates as the old loop bodies.

**Divide.** The halving replay reads the tape in walk order.
1. Walk rounds `0..prefix` alone. No coefficient is allocated yet.
2. Allocate `coefficient` and batch-replay rounds `0..prefix` with budget
   `fused_round_budget(prefix, prefix)`.
3. For each `round` in `prefix..700`, run `walk_round(round)` and then
   `replay_halving_round(round)`, both with budget
   `fused_round_budget(round, round + 1)`.
4. Canonicalise both registers with `conditional_mod_negate_budget`, clear the
   duplicate with CNOTs, free `coefficient`, and run the plain
   `value_walk_back`.

**Multiply.** The doubling-inverse replay reads the tape in walk-back order.
1. Run the full `value_walk`, allocate `coefficient`, copy the numerator into
   it, and do the two budgeted terminal negates.
2. For `round` from 699 down to `prefix`, run `replay_doubling_inverse_round`
   and then `walk_back_round`. The walk-back frees that round's sign at once.
3. Replay rounds `prefix−1..0`, free `coefficient`, then walk back
   `prefix−1..0` and regrow the registers (`walk_back_finish`).

The fused core has no terminal wire loans. The base's 1-bit loan is kept only
in the legacy core.

### 2. The budget model

- `fused_live_floor(round, tape_live) = 2·256 + tape_live + 2·value_width(round) + 3`.
  This counts both coefficient registers, the tape so far, both walk
  registers at the scheduled width, the round's sign and some slack.
- `fused_round_budget(round, tape_live) = max(QSTAR − floor, REPLAY_CHUNK_COMPARE + 10)`.
  With `QSTAR = 1283` and `REPLAY_CHUNK_COMPARE = 23`, the budget never goes
  below 33.
- The floor rises with `round`. The tape gains 1 wire per round, and the two
  walk registers together lose only 0.66 to 0.8 wires per round
  (`SLOPE_2 = 33`, `SLOPE_3 = 40` per 100 rounds each). So early
  rounds get large budgets and the last rounds get about 50.
- `fused_prefix()` picks the smallest `prefix` that meets two conditions. It
  can be overridden with `SUB4_PP_FUSED_PREFIX`.
  - Every later walk add must fit its budget unsplit
    (`value_width(r) − 2 ≤ budget`).
  - The batch must still have a budget of 33 or more.
- With the default `value_width`, the code's formula gives **prefix 492**
  (width 92, batch budget 92). The floor is 1191 at the batch and 1233 at
  round 699, where the budget is 50.

### 3. Narrow-scratch variants, picked by budget

- **Replay add** (`budgeted_pair_add`).
  - With a budget of 102 or more, it is the stock `add_chunked_measured`
    (chunk limit 96).
  - With a budget of 31 to 101, it is the same chunked add with chunk width
    `min(budget − 4, 96)`. That gives more chunks and more boundary carries.
    Each boundary carry is erased by `hmr` and repaired with a 23-bit
    truncated `cmp_lt_phase_conditioned`.
  - Below 31 it would be an exact CDKM ripple (`add_ripple_exact`). That
    never fires at the defaults.
  - In the fused band (rounds 492..699), the chunk width falls from 87 to 45.
    An add there has 3 to 6 chunks (2 to 5 approximate boundaries) instead of
    3 chunks.
- **Fold** (`budgeted_fold`).
  - It triggers when the budget is below `REPLAY_FOLD_WINDOW + flags + 2`.
    That is 54 + 7 + 2 = 63 for the halving cell. The doubling cell uses
    flags = 9.
  - The mask-free fold is then replaced by the new helper
    `selected_const_add_segmented`. This is a Gidney chain over the fold
    window, cut into segments of at most `budget − flags − 2` carries.
  - Carries inside a segment are erased exactly by `hmr` plus `cz_if`. Each
    kept segment boundary is erased by `hmr` and repaired with a 23-bit
    truncated compare against the rematerialised operand window.
  - By the formula, this applies to about 67 rounds in the divide traversal
    (rounds 631..699) and 77 in the multiply traversal (rounds 621..699).
- **Flag compare** (`budgeted_flag_compare`). Below
  `REPLAY_FLAG_COMPARE + 6` it switches to the constant-scratch compare. That
  never fires at the defaults, because the smallest budget is 49.
- **Terminal negate** (`conditional_mod_negate_budget`). It computes
  `p − x = NOT(x + (f − 1))` through the segmented helper with
  `max_seg = budget − 4 = 46`. The base used one unsegmented chain of about
  87 carries here, at the full-tape moment.
- **Round-0/1 halving and doubling** (`mod_halve_pm_budget`,
  `mod_double_pm_budget`, `seed_round_one(_inverse)_budget`). They use
  `csub/cadd_ctrl_const_trunc_budget` when the budget is below
  33 + `ENDPOINT_FOLD_WINDOW` + 4 = 91. Rounds 0 and 1 are replayed in the
  prefix batch with budget 92, so the defaults keep the stock versions.
- **Walk add** (`budgeted_walk_add`).
  - If the budget is below `width − 2`, the add is split into two halves.
    The boundary is repaired exactly, by a constant-scratch compare over the
    whole low half.
  - `fused_prefix` is chosen so that this never fires at the defaults.
  - A code comment explains why a truncated boundary compare is not used on
    the walk: the walk values are sign-extension runs, so a top-bits compare
    ties with probability ½.
- **New primitive `cmp_lt_phase_conditioned_slow`**.
  - It is a conditioned in-place majority ripple (`maj` / `inv_maj`) on the
    compared registers.
  - It costs 2 Toffoli per position, about 1 executed under the ½
    condition, and needs one scratch wire. The fast compare needs one wire
    per position.
  - It is used for the segment boundaries.

### 4. Keeping the square off the peak

When fused mode is on, `build_pingpong_point_add` sets the default
`SUB4_SQUARE_ADD_CAP = 1253`.
- In `trailmix_ludicrous/square/product_register.rs`, `add_full` reads this
  value and passes `max(cap − active_qubits, 48)` to `hybrid_add_adaptive` as
  its vent budget. Before, that budget was unbounded.
- The code comment says that once the replay crest is lower, the square's
  unbounded vents would become the peak.
- With the variable unset, the add is unchanged.

### 5. Other edits

- The tail nonce (`SUB4_PINGPONG_TAIL_NONCE` default in `src/point_add/mod.rs`)
  goes from 191412 to 3200265.
- Debug counters (`DBG_COST`, `dbg_report`, `PP_DBG`) print but emit no gates.

**Why it saves qubits.** In the base, all 1,400 replay rounds share the floor
`700 + 512 + walk + ladder`. Here, replay rounds before 492 run with a tape of
at most 492 wires, and later rounds shrink their own scratch to fit. The high
point is the last few rounds, with about 50 scratch wires over a floor of
1233. The measured peak is 1281.

**Where the +10.7k Toffoli goes.** These figures are estimated from the code's
budget formula.
- The fused band adds about 326 approximate chunk boundaries per traversal
  over the baseline of 2 per round. At about 11.4 executed Toffoli per 23-bit
  repair (the figure 0xrlawrence measured in `f3b2f1f`'s note), that is about
  7.4k for both traversals.
- The roughly 144 segmented folds add about 124 slow 23-bit segment repairs
  between them, which is about 3k more.
- The vent cap on the square adds a little more.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **Exact parts.**
  - The reorder changes when each round runs, not what it computes. Replay
    round `r` reads only `tape[r]`. The divide replay reads the tape in walk
    order and the multiply replay in walk-back order, so interleaving keeps
    every round's input.
  - Carries inside a segment are erased by `hmr` plus `cz_if`, which is exact.
    See [measurement-uncompute](../concepts/measurement-uncompute.md).
  - The ripple and walk-split paths are exact but unused at the defaults.
- **Probabilistic parts.** These are more instances of an existing
  truncation class, not a new kind of approximation:
  - the extra chunk boundaries in the fused band, each repaired with a 23-bit
    truncated compare;
  - the segment boundaries in the late folds and in the terminal negate, also
    23-bit truncated compares.
- A wrong repair leaves the value right and the phase wrong, so it shows up on
  the phase channel. The window widths are unchanged. See
  [comparator-and-width-truncation](../concepts/comparator-and-width-truncation.md).
- More repairs raise λ a little. The shipped nonce `3200265` draws a clean
  set: 9,024 of 9,024 shots, with 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla
  failures. See [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The qubit saving comes from changing which registers are live together. Each
late replay round is moved next to its walk round, so most rounds do not share
the machine with the full tape. That is a lifetime change: ancilla-lifetime /
venting (see "Rescheduling" in
[venting-and-wire-loans](../concepts/venting-and-wire-loans.md)). The narrow
arithmetic variants exist only to make the late rounds fit. The runner-up was
scratch/ancilla reuse, but no wire is lent or parked. "Fused" here means round
interleaving, not Fusion in the taxonomy's sense.

   The flag lets the patch apply whether the files use LF or CRLF line endings.

   `set_default_env` inside the builder. Expect:
   - 1281 qubits
   - 950,724 average Toffoli (rounded)
   - score 1,217,877,444
   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures
