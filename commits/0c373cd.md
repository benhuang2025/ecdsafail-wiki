## Summary

q70c lowers the walk cap `HEO_PIN_PP_WALK_MAX_QUBITS` from 1171 to 1170,
and the peak follows. To make the walk fit and to pay for the lost room, the
commit does six things:

1. **Head trim (`R0_HEAD_TRIM=1,0`).** During the multiply leg's head batch,
   rail R1 is held one wire narrower. Its top wire is assumed to be a sign
   copy, uncomputed and freed, then sign-extended back before the reverse
   rail pass. The head-batch cells get that wire as room. The author
   measured −102 T for +0.015 ± 0.013 λ for this alone.
2. **A new per-cell bridge/room table** (`q70c_k3b_retune.txt`, 369 cells),
   re-picked for the 1170 cap.
3. **A larger need table** (`q70c_need.txt`, 90 cells, replacing q71d's 32).
   This is the same mechanism as [`a6034cb`](a6034cb.md): lower the
   retained-fold workspace need so more late cells take the cheaper retained
   route.
4. **Low-cut sells** on the retained prebias (`GO_PRB`) and the joint low
   fold (`GO_JLB`).
5. **Compare buys** on about 186 cells (`GO_CELLB`).
6. **A re-ported rewrite table** of only 104 rows (q71d had 345).

The net is +714 Toffoli at one qubit fewer, which lowers the score by
110,459. The author reports that the sells and buys together lower λ by
0.129 ± 0.028 against a build with only the cap, the head trim and the rows.
The final λ is 19.284 ± 0.032.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

### 1. Walk cap: 1171 → 1170 (`mod.rs`, q70c block)

Every chunked add and fit add sized under `walk_max_qubits() −
active_qubits()` loses a wire of room. See
[peak-qubits-and-budgets](../concepts/peak-qubits-and-budgets.md).

### 2. `R0_HEAD_TRIM=1,0` (`heo_carry.rs`, `multiply`)

The new code sits in the RB-1 single-head-batch path (`lt0m`).

1. **Before the head batch:** it reads `R0_HEAD_TRIM=a,b` and calls
   `resize(rails.r1, hw1 − a)` and `resize(rails.r2, hw2 − b)`.
   - `heo::resize` trims a register by "CX from the new top + free (the
     trimmed wire must be a sign copy: the envelope's promise)".
   - With `1,0`, the top wire of R1 is freed and R2 is untouched.
2. **After the head batch and the `Del` free, before `heo_mul_railsrev2`:**
   both rails are resized back to their old widths. Growing is a
   sign-extension (alloc + CX from the top).

The code comment says the trim is "correct only where they are sign copies".
So the trim adds a width promise on the rails at tick `HEO_R1M` (94). An
input whose R1 there needs its top bit would leave a nonzero wire freed.
While the head batch runs, every head-batch cell sees one more wire of room.
That is where the −102 T comes from. See
[venting-and-wire-loans](../concepts/venting-and-wire-loans.md).

The commit also adds three hooks that the recipe does not set, so they are
inert:

- `R0_MID_TRIM` and `R0_MID_PAD` would trim or pad the rails around
  mid-tick cells, called from `fama_fwd` / `fama_rev`;
- `R0_RAW_FLUSH` would pack3 a division batch group early.

### 3. Per-cell bridge/room table: `q71a_k3b_retune.txt` (226) → `q70c_k3b_retune.txt` (369)

The `include_str!` in `k3b_cell` switches files.

- The new table has 227 division and 142 multiply rows.
- Bridge deltas: 302 × +0, 26 × +1, 17 × +2, 1 × +3, 23 × −1.
- Room deltas run from −10 to +5, and 174 rows are negative.

The bridge deltas change each cell's I35 bridge count, which trades CCX for
carry wires. The room deltas move that cell's own cap. See
[`9db31da`](9db31da.md) for the format.

### 4. Need table: `q71d_need.txt` (32 cells) → `q70c_need.txt` (90 cells)

The loader and comment in `k3b_cell` switch files.

- The new table covers cell indices 347–442. Adjustments run from 2 to 16
  and sum to 699; q71d's summed to 263.
- The mechanism is unchanged. `S5_NEED_ADJ` lowers `need` in
  `retained_prebias::plan` (division) and `joint_lowfold::plan` (multiply).
  The prefix carry split and its erase compare shrink, and the shortfall
  goes to the exact `direct_plan` split. See [`a6034cb`](a6034cb.md).
- The author re-optimised the table per cell at the 1170 cap and calls it a
  sell.

### 5. Low-cut sells: `GO_PRB` and `GO_JLB` (q70c block)

Both are read through `go_slice`, first match wins, by proxy round.

| knob | before | after | what it narrows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GO_PRB` | `631-698:-1` | `631-698:-4,500-569:-1` | `bits` of the retained-prebias low cut (base `PP_PREBIAS_RETAIN_BITS` = 32): 31 → 28 at rounds 631–698, 32 → 31 at rounds 500–569 |
| `GO_JLB` | `616-698:m:-1` | `616-698:m:-3` | the multiply joint-low-fold `low` bits (base `PP_JOINT_LOW_BITS` = 32): 31 → 29 at rounds 616–698 |

A narrower low cut is cheaper. The plan's `saving` term includes
`−(bits − 33)`. It is also less accurate. See
[modular-fold](../concepts/modular-fold.md).

### 6. Compare buys: `GO_CELLB` (q70c block)

The q71d list gains three groups:

- 36 single-tick `+1` entries on multiply ticks 355–390;
- `div:83-164:1` (82 cells);
- `mul:165-232:1` (68 cells).

`go_cell_delta` sums all matching entries, so the net shifts are:

- multiply 369–390: 0 (they had −1);
- multiply 360 and 361: +2;
- multiply 165–194: +2 (with `mul:105-194:1`);
- division 150–155: 0 (they had −1).

dB widens the chunk-boundary erase compares of those cells. The submission
note says the 355–390 band came from the unsubmitted q71e2 and the two wide
bands are new. See
[comparator-and-width-truncation](../concepts/comparator-and-width-truncation.md).

### 7. Rewrite rows: `q71d_rewrite.txt` (345) → `q70c_rewrite.txt` (104)

- The comment reads "104 rows on the q70c op stream (p70 census, 2M-shot
  survivors with NEGCTL)".
- The submission note says the q71e2 rows were ported by op-window matching,
  plus 30 new rows from a census over windows where rows were lost. Each new
  row "passed about 2M shots per CCX with 0 fails" with a failing negative
  control.
- In the file, 99 rows are in ops 4.5M–5.3M, 3 are earlier and 2 later.
- 241 fewer rows than q71d put back about 241 CCX per shot. That is a large
  part of the +714.

### 8. Tail nonce

`TAIL_NONCE` goes from 30000833880904 to 32500104792978.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **Exact parts.** The cap, the bridge/room table and the `direct_plan`
  split change layout only.
- **Probabilistic parts.**
  - **The head trim** is a width promise. An input whose R1 top bit at tick
    94 is not a sign copy frees a nonzero wire. That shows up as an
    **ancilla** failure, and the wrong rail value can also give a
    **classical** failure. The author measured +0.015 ± 0.013 λ.
  - **The need table and the `GO_PRB` / `GO_JLB` sells** move more cells
    onto the finite low-cut fold and narrow that cut. Their errors show up
    as **classical** failures.
  - **The `GO_CELLB` buys** halve the tie error of the widened erases,
    which lowers **phase** failures.
  - **The new census rows** are not SAT-proved. They passed about 2M shots
    each.
- **λ.** The submission note gives 19.284 ± 0.032 on 16,384 nonces (est + 2 SE
  19.348). That is lower than q71d's 19.318, because the buys outweigh the
  sells. Mean T on that run is 946,543.50.
- The shipped nonce `32500104792978` draws a clean island: 0 classical,
  0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The commit exists to take one qubit off: a lower cap, a trimmed rail
through the head batch, and a re-fitted per-cell room table. That is
ancilla-lifetime / venting. The λ trades (need table, low-cut sells, compare
buys) keep the error budget level, and the rewrite port is maintenance.

## Reproduce

1. **Start from the base.** Clone https://github.com/ecdsafail/ecdsafail-challenge
   and run `git checkout a6034cb -- src/point_add`.
   - The base is the git parent. The submission note says q70c was built
     on the unsubmitted q71e2 settings, whose need table starts with q71d's
     32 entries. q71e2 was never committed, so q71d is the nearest accepted
     tree.
   - `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol a6034cb 0c373cd -- src/point_add` is 1,033
     lines in 7 files.
2. **Delete the two q71d tables and copy the new ones.** Delete them with
   the `rm` command under Patch below. Then copy from the commit:
   `git checkout 0c373cd -- src/point_add/skywalk_data/q70c_k3b_retune.txt src/point_add/skywalk_data/q70c_rewrite.txt`.
   These are the 369-row bridge/room table and the 104 rewrite rows
   described above.
3. **Apply the patch below** from the repo root with `git apply --ignore-whitespace`.
   The flag lets the patch apply whether the files use LF or CRLF line
   endings. It covers `heo_carry.rs`, `mod.rs` and the new `q70c_need.txt`.
4. **Check the tree.** `git diff --ignore-cr-at-eol 0c373cd -- src/point_add`
   should print nothing.
5. **Run the benchmark.** Run `./setup.sh && ./benchmark.sh`. `build()` clears
   the process environment and then installs the pinned recipe, so `HEO_*`,
   `GO_*`, `SKY_*`, `S5_*`, `R0_*`, `PP_*` and `SUB4_*` overrides have no
   effect. Do not set them anyway. Expect:
   - 1170 qubits
   - 946,553 average Toffoli (946,553.458 before rounding, by the submission
     note)
   - score 1,107,467,010
   - 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures

The score only reproduces with the nonce `32500104792978` and an identical
op stream.
