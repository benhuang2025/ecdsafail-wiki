## Summary

At 1226 qubits the peak was shared by the apply-phase fused double/halve fold,
the windowed λ² square, and the apply add ripple. This commit lowers all three:

- **Fold.** The fold ripple of `dialog_gcd_fused_double_y` /
  `dialog_gcd_fused_halve_y` keeps eight small control and overflow wires
  alive while its carry chain runs up through the pure-propagation tail above
  bit 33. Four of those wires (`h`, `xed`, `eord`, `n10`) are not needed in
  the tail. The new freed-tail ripple releases them before the tail carries
  are allocated and rebuilds them afterwards, so the fold's high-water drops
  by 4. Rebuilding `h` costs one Toffoli per call.
- **Square.** The window width of the square goes from 199 to 194, so five
  more rows are split into two windows.
- **Apply ripple.** It is cut into 11 chunks instead of 10.

The peak falls to 1221 at a cost of +2,979 Toffoli. The fold change is exact;
the other two only move carries around. The nonce was re-hunted.

## What changed and why it saves qubits

1. **Freed-tail fold (`DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_FREED_TAIL=1`).**
   - **Setting.** The fused fold adds `δ = c·e + 2c·d`, with
     `c = 2^32 + 977`, into `y`. It uses per-position controls built from
     `e`, `d` and four derived wires: `h = e&d`, `xed = e⊕d`, `eord = e|d` and
     `n10 = ¬e&d` (`secp_fold_controls`).
   - Controls exist only at bit positions ≤ 33. Positions 34 up to
     `last = 33 + KAL_DOUBLE_CARRY_TRUNC_W` (= 52) only propagate a carry.
     The base `cadd/csub_per_position_controls_trunc` allocates the whole carry
     lane, `last + 1` wires, at once, while all derived controls are alive.
   - **New `fold_ripple_freed_tail(b, acc, e, d, h, xed, eord, n10, last, is_add)`**
     in `arith/const_arith.rs`:
     1. It allocates only the low carries `low[0..=33]` and runs the active
        region with the same majority gates (`emit_fold_majority`).
     2. It writes the low sum bits while the controls are alive.
     3. It frees `n10`, `eord` and `xed` by undoing their CNOTs, and clears
        `h` by `hmr(h) + cz_if(e, d)`, at 0 Toffoli.
     4. Only then does it allocate the 19 tail carries. It generates them from
        the original high bits of `acc`, with control-free CCX, and writes the
        high sum bits.
     5. It erases the tail carries by measurement, high to low, and frees them.
     6. It re-acquires the same four wires and recomputes them:
        `ccx(e, d, h)` plus CNOTs.
     7. It erases the low carries by measurement as before.
   - At return, `h`, `xed`, `eord` and `n10` are live and hold the same values
     as after the base ripple, so the caller's cleanup is unchanged.
   - **Effect.** While the tail is being processed, 4 fewer wires are live. The
     cost is the one recomputed `ccx(e, d, h)`, +1 Toffoli per call. That is
     about +516 over the 2 × 258 apply doubles and halves (the author says
     about +511).
   - **Wiring.** `rounds/dialog/compressed.rs` calls the new ripple in both
     `dialog_gcd_fused_double_y` (add) and `dialog_gcd_fused_halve_y`
     (subtract) when the switch is on and `last > 33`.
   - Also added, and unused on this route: `DIALOG_GCD_FOLD_CARRY_TRUNC_W`
     (`fold_only_carry_trunc_window`), a separate carry window for the fused
     fold only. When it is unset, `last` falls back to `KAL_DOUBLE_CARRY_TRUNC_W`
     as before.
2. **`SQUARE_ROW_MAX_SEG`: 199 → 194.**
   - In the peak-bounded schoolbook square of [`7689eac`](7689eac.md), rows
     wider than this are added in `ceil(width/194)` windows, so the transient
     source register is at most 194 wires.
   - Row `i` has width `257 − i`, so rows 0–62 are now windowed instead of rows
     0–57. Each still uses 2 windows.
   - The narrower segment lowers the square's peak, and each extra windowed row
     pays the carry-recovery comparator.
3. **`DIALOG_GCD_APPLY_CHUNKED_F_BLOCKS`: 10 → 11.** The apply add/sub ripple
   is cut into 11 even chunks of about 23 bits instead of about 26. That
   narrows its transient register, at the cost of one more boundary comparator
   per call.
4. **`DIALOG_TAIL_NONCE`**: 2000004892833 → 165002130437.
5. **Self-test.** `fold_freed_tail_selftest()` in `src/point_add/mod.rs` runs
   when `FOLD_FREED_TAIL_SELFTEST` is set in `build()`. For each `(e, d)`, add
   and subtract, windowed and unwindowed, it builds the base ripple and the
   freed-tail ripple on 64 random `y` values (including all-ones carry runs),
   simulates both, and checks equal outputs, clean ancillae and zero phase. It
   emits no ops in a normal build.

**Peak bookkeeping.** The author reports that afterwards the fold sits at 1220
and the apply ripple at 1220 or below. The new peak of 1221 is shared by the
windowed square and the round84 in-place Solinas fold/unfold, which the author
calls the wall for the next step down.

**Toffoli bookkeeping.** About +516 comes from the recomputed `h`. The rest,
about +2,460, comes from the five extra windowed square rows (both in the
square and in its inverse) and the extra apply chunk boundary. This split is
an estimate.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **The freed-tail ripple is exact.**
  - The tail carries depend only on `acc` and the carry from bit 33, never on
    the controls, so freeing the controls cannot change them.
  - The controls are rebuilt from `e` and `d`, which the ripple does not touch.
  - `h` is cleared with `hmr` + `cz_if(e, d)`, which is phase-exact because
    `h = e & d` at that moment.
  - The self-test checks this against the base ripple.
- **Square windowing and apply chunking are exact.** They change where carries
  live, not the sum.
- **Probabilistic parts.** No new truncation is added. The inherited ones are
  unchanged, but the op stream changed, so the test set is new.
- The shipped nonce `165002130437` draws a clean set: 9,024 of 9,024 shots,
  with 0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. The note says it took 38
  GCD-clean candidates, about 9 minutes on 16 GPUs.
- The submission note calls the lever a "round84 Solinas-fold" change. In the
  code it is the apply-phase fused double/halve fold of the dialog GCD.

## Classification rationale

The main lever shortens the lifetime of four ancillae: they are freed while
not needed and recomputed later. That is ancilla-lifetime / venting. The
square-window and chunk-count changes are retunes of existing windowing knobs
that move two co-binders under the new peak.

+                }
