## Summary

In `f3b2f1f`'s interleaved plan, each replay add picks a chunk *count*, 3 or
4, and splits 256 bits evenly. Each chunk boundary carry is erased by
measurement and repaired with a compare of the top 23 bits, which is only
approximate.

This commit asks for a ladder *budget* in qubits instead. A new search,
`chunk_layout`, returns the layout that fits the budget with the fewest
approximate repairs. Its key trick is to make the first chunk at most 23 bits
wide. Chunk 0 has no carry-in, so a 23-bit compare then covers the whole chunk
and the repair is exact. A narrow exact leading chunk lets the remaining wide
chunks be smaller for the same number of approximate repairs.

With that, the target peak goes from 1279 to 1278 and the batch checkpoint
moves from 503 to 509. The square's wide adds get their own 248-wire budget,
which turns their two approximate repairs into one exact one. The result is −1
qubit and −232 Toffoli.

## What changed and why it saves a qubit

All edits are in `src/point_add/pingpong_div.rs` unless noted.

### 1. Chunk layout by ladder budget

- **Thread-local.** `CHUNK_OVERRIDE` (a chunk width) is replaced by
  `LADDER_TARGET` (a live-ladder budget in qubits). Its accessors are
  `set_ladder`, `ladder_target_now` and `clear_chunks`.
  - The old behaviour is kept behind `SUB4_PP_LEGACY_LADDER`.
  - In legacy mode, `set_chunks_width` stores `usize::MAX − width`, and
    `legacy_width` decodes it.
- **Budget per round.** `pick_chunks` now returns
  `ladder_for_allowance(a, extra) = a − extra`. Here `a` is the same allowance
  as in `f3b2f1f`: `peak − (tape + 512 + 2·walk_width)`. `extra` is 1 for the
  multiply cell.
- **Footprint model.** `chunk_live(j, k, w, final_carry)` is the live
  footprint of chunk `j`: the incoming boundary carry (if `j > 0`), the
  outgoing one (if there is a next chunk or a carry-out), and `w − 1` Gidney
  carries. `layout_ladder` is the maximum over all chunks.
- **`chunk_layout(n, target, final_carry)`.** It tries `wide = 0, 1, …, 12`
  approximate boundaries, cheapest first:
  - (a) An equal split into `wide + 1` chunks, where every boundary is
    approximate.
  - (b) A leading chunk of at most `REPLAY_CHUNK_COMPARE = 23` bits, plus
    `wide + 1` chunks sized to the budget. The leading chunk is shrunk first,
    then the top chunks.
  - It returns the first layout whose ladder fits. If none fits, it falls back
    to 12 equal chunks.
- **Why the leading chunk is exact.** `erase` in `add_chunked_measured_with`
  compares the top `min(23, w)` bits of the chunk that produced the carry. For
  a chunk of 23 bits or fewer, that is the whole chunk. Chunk 0 has no
  carry-in, so its carry-out is exactly `sum < addend` over the chunk.
- **Wiring.** `add_chunked_measured_with` computes
  `chunk_layout(n, budget, final_carry)` when a budget is set. With no budget
  it uses the stock `chunk_bounds(n, 96)`.

Worked examples from the code's rule, for a 256-bit replay add with
carry-out:

| budget | f3b2f1f (count) | e928607 (`chunk_layout`) | approximate repairs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 86 | 3 × ~85 | [86, 85, 85] | 2 → 2 |
| 79–85 | 4 × 64 | [≤23, 78…84 × 3] | 3 → **2** |
| 65–78 | 4 × 64 | [64, 64, 64, 64] | 3 → 3 |
| 64 | 5 × ~51 | [4, 63, 63, 63, 63] | 4 → 3 |

At budget 64, the new layout gives the same number of approximate repairs as
`f3b2f1f`'s 4 chunks, with one fewer ladder wire.

### 2. Retuned plan: `SUB4_PP_PEAK` 1279 → 1278, `SUB4_PP_R1` 503 → 509

`SUB4_PP_R2` stays at 610.

- **Divide.** Before the batch replay, the walk registers are shrunk to
  `value_width(r1)` (`shrink_to`). `walk_round(r1)` would do this anyway, so
  the op count is the same, but the batch carries 2 fewer wires.
- **Both directions.** The batch replay now runs under a ladder budget,
  `pick_chunks(plan, r1, u.len())`, instead of the stock chunking (limit 96). With the
  code's formula and `value_width(509) = 85`, that budget is
  `1278 − 509 − 512 − 170 = 87`, which still fits 3 equal chunks.
- **Tail batch.** The allowance at the loaned terminal state is now
  `1278 − 700 − 512 − 2 = 64` in the divide and 64 in the multiply
  (tape 699, extra 1). It takes the [4, 63, 63, 63, 63] layout: still 3
  approximate repairs, with one wire less than `f3b2f1f`'s 65.
- **Interleaved band.** By the code's formula, about 30 rounds per traversal
  have budgets of 79–85. They drop from 3 approximate repairs to 2.

### 3. Square: `SQUARE_LADDER = 248` (`trailmix_ludicrous/square/product_register.rs`)

- In `f3b2f1f`, `add_full` sends adds of `SQUARE_CHUNK_MIN = 200` bits or more
  to `add_chunked_measured` with the default chunking (limit 96). That is 3 chunks
  and 2 approximate repairs.
- It now calls the new `add_chunked_measured_budgeted(…, budget)`, with budget
  `SUB4_SQUARE_LADDER`, default 248. A budget of 0 restores the old call.
- For a 256-bit add with no carry-out, `chunk_layout(256, 248, false)` gives
  `[8, 248]`: one boundary, repaired exactly. A 257-bit add gives `[9, 248]`.
- The code comment says the square's own footprint sits about 160 qubits below
  the replay peak, so it can afford the wide ladder.

### 4. Nonce

`src/point_add/mod.rs`: the tail nonce goes from 3004060 to 6215000001.

**Why the peak drops.** The binding phases in `f3b2f1f` were the tail batch at
`700 + 512 + 2 + 65 = 1279` and the interleaved and batch rounds at 3 or 4
equal chunks. With the leading-chunk layouts, every replay add fits a budget
one wire smaller with no extra approximate repairs. Then `PEAK = 1278` holds
everywhere.

**Why Toffoli moves only a little.** The exact leading repair is not free: it
is still a phase-conditioned compare, over the leading chunk's `w0 ≤ 23` bits.
- A layout such as [22, 78, 78, 78] has as many boundaries as
  [64, 64, 64, 64]. It turns one 23-bit truncated compare into a 22-bit exact
  one, so it saves λ but almost no Toffoli.
- The real Toffoli savings come from layouts whose leading chunk is shrunk to a
  few bits, such as [4, 84, 84, 84] or [4, 63, 63, 63, 63], and from the
  square. There, two 23-bit compares become one 8- or 9-bit compare per wide
  add.
- The net measured change is −232.

## Why it is still correct, and what it risks

- **Exact parts.**
  - The leading-chunk repair compares the whole chunk, and chunk 0 has no
    carry-in, so it is an identity. This also holds for the square's new
    single boundary.
  - Inside each chunk, carries are uncomputed by measurement as before. See
    [measurement-uncompute](../concepts/measurement-uncompute.md).
  - The batch shrink is the same CX-and-free that `walk_round(r1)` would do.
- **Probabilistic parts.** The remaining wide boundaries use the same 23-bit
  truncated compare as before. By the code's layout rule, there are fewer of
  them per shot than in the base: the square loses two per wide add, and about
  60 replay rounds per point add lose one each. So λ should not rise. A wrong repair would show up as a phase failure.
  See [comparator-and-width-truncation](../concepts/comparator-and-width-truncation.md).
- The shipped nonce `6215000001` draws a clean set: 9,024 of 9,024 shots, with
  0 classical, 0 phase and 0 ancilla failures. See
  [nonce-island-and-lambda](../concepts/nonce-island-and-lambda.md).

## Classification rationale

The score gain is the qubit. It comes from re-budgeting the measured-erasure
carry ladders of the chunked adders and from shrinking the walk registers
before the batch. That is a change to how much scratch is live at the crest,
so it is ancilla-lifetime / venting, in line with the other interleave-plan
commits. The exact leading chunk could also be read as a comparator change,
but it removes a truncation instead of narrowing one. The Toffoli saving is a
side effect.
